This letter, many traditional scholars believed that it’s written for the Christians in Ephesians when Paul was under house arrest in Rome after bring a gentile to the temple. As the original text lacked the “who are in Ephesus and are faithful”, it is also believed that this letter is written for all Christians in general. There is simply no way to prove it now except by assumptions, deductions and when the day comes we all meet together in the Lord that the truth will be revealed not that I think this would take any priority among everything else if it does. Though the history cannot be accurately determined, the context of this letter is still clear, it’s a concept of a Church. 

Paul started by warm greetings and prayers of blessings & affirmation which speaks a lot of how Paul actually felt towards the Ephesians. In this letter, he expressed through his words when he thought of them, he was filled with gratitude, and he wanted to bless them more so that they may know Christ more. It seems that he’s teaching them the fundamentals as a new believer and it matches the timeline as they were just beginning to assemble as a Church of Christ. They would need a mature leader to guide them and lead them, we in this era of the year 2026, Oct 26th, are blessed to have the speed and technology to be able to learn from different teachers around the whole earth in an instant click. However it may also be used as a tool for the false teachers hence we are also reminded to beware of false teachings and the authenticity of teachers by examining their fruits. So in Ephesians, Paul the apostle cared much about them and wrote the letter to them as he was in house arrest. Constraint yet free in Christ to preach boldly of the gospel of Christ. It’s a perfect example for us believers who feels that this world is full of rules and chains that tried to control us from living the way that Christ want us to live, they may try to control our speech, our voices, our freedom physically, but in spirit and in our heart, we are free to worship God, our Lord Jesus Christ, the one true God, the truth and the way. 

Take some time to read carefully each word in Paul’s prayer and let each sentence fill our mind and heart, even better let it be a prayer each day. It is a call for us to continue to believe in Christ and focus on Him as we come to know Him as time goes by. We must put some effort in studying the bible and prayers, but also to allow ourselves to have some space just to slow down and notice what God is doing in our lives. That will help us to practice gratitude and worship God authentically.
