In this chapter, Paul was trying to address the issue of verse 10 where someone had accused him of being too harsh and inflict fear on the Corinthians. His tone here reminds me of Moses, another human being that worship God and love God who said that they are humble. For years, a deception that I had was humility is having low self value or just denied and downplay every praise or credit because that was how it was shown in the church. It created an unhealthy relationship with God and myself and it hurt also the way that I perceive others when they are truly being humble. The best word in existence to describe humility is being unpretentious. So when one knows if credit is due, then just receive it gracefully but project also the thanksgiving towards God who enabled or given this chance for the success. 

There was a time when a sister had to confront another sister J who God gave the chance to bring her to Christ and converted to Christian by leading her to say the sinner’s prayer and arranged her to go to a church. Alas, she was a devil at home and an angel at church, it was heartbreaking, then when the conversation got heated at one of the confrontation, the sister spoke to J saying “I’m not perfect but I humble myself before God knowing that what you did was wrong and I called it out as it is” and J scoffed :“no one who is humble will say that they are humble”. The sister knew that she heard this from the church because that was how the sermon was preached and taught in that particular denomination. I knew then this very church that I’ve left years ago because of how corrupted they were did not change at all and then I understood and could empathized a little bit more with Moses and Paul. I realized no matter what I said, it will always be fruitless with this sister. J had learned the ways of the church not to become Christlike but to twist it all around and navigate in between the loopholes with her wordplays. Every interaction was her trying to get more ammunition to come against others who walk in truth. 

It was disheartening to experience all that, alas, even Jesus Christ had a Judas. It was one of those experience that led me to understand more about our Lord Jesus Christ. It made me appreciate and hold on to God even more. Paul forgave first and then he set a boundary when some of the Corinthians refused to repent. It is the only way to stay focus on the goal that God has given him, that’s what the devil tried to do, seeping away his strength and emotional capacity on some division or confusion so that he lost sight of the goal. However God is gracious, He protected Paul and provided for him to be able to finish the mission he was suppose to accomplished. The context in this chapter is that Paul did not boast beyond what God has given to him, he was the first to bring the good news to the Corinthians and they had not follow his teachings but had succumbed to false teachings and he wanted to wake them up, this is what he meant by the divine power to destroy strongholds. He used letters and words to try and break those strongholds of lies and to help the Corinthians see the truth. 

May our Lord Jesus Christ help us all to be able to stand bold against falsehood in where He had sent us and not be deceived but to speak the truth in order to lead people to God.
