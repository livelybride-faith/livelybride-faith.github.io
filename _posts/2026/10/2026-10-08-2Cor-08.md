title: 2 Cor 8 - Genuineness of love towards God

This is yet another chapter where Paul’s view is truly different but aligned with God’s perspective, especially how he commended the churches of Macedonia, he did not focus and grieved on the severe affliction that happened to them but he saw that they had abundant of joy and how truly generous they were in the contrast of their extreme poverty. The worldly view would deem them useless as to how much could a poor person give? However just like how Jesus Christ taught us, He valued the heart and the intention of the giver for example a widow gave all her possession out of her poverty. Paul described the churches of Macedonia in that similar manner with different wordings. 

Paul knew that our Lord Jesus Christ focus only on the genuineness of one’s love, maybe it’s to prepare the church of Corinthians so that they give generously not for the sake of performance or for the fear of losing their reputation but truly from a pure heart of genuine sincerity and love. He would rather they do not give if it’s purely out of obligation. Even so, he did not stop them entirely but try to explain to them why giving is still the ultimate quality and standard of being a Christian. This is the way of Christ, how we show love towards each other to give to those who has less so that they do not too little and those who have much do not have too much. Do not be fooled by deceptions, God sees the heart and He knows who is a Cain and who is an Abel, and God too spoke to Cain to be alert and watchful for sin is crouching at the door, in order that Cain can stop sinning and repent. Alas he killed Abel just because Abel shown genuine love. Let us take heed that this is not just a story in the bible long long time ago but it’s moral story and principles still does applies to us, the follower of Christ as of this day and in the future. 

Paul is one of those teachers that did not splash cold water on the passion and eagerness of someone who just want to show love, in contrast to those who are jealous of God, just because believers pour out of all them towards God, that they feel that it’s cringed. To God, that is not cringed but an eagerness that can only be shown in such a limited way of human expression. To the world, it is cringe because they prefer stoic emotionless restraint especially when someone is watching. To the true believers of Christ, it is an expression of love towards God. How blessed one is to be able to see and share that glimpse of outflowing love towards God with such sweetness and awe from one’s heart. 

May our Lord Jesus Christ help us all to be able to love God and pour out our hearts towards Him according to our means genuinely without any mask but with genuineness of our love.
