Paul’s statement regarding the “heirs according to the promise” was explained extensively in this chapter so that his fellow Jews brothers and sisters together with the Galatians gentiles brothers and sisters will be able to understand the purpose of the Torah, the Mosaic law. It was not that it was useless and ineffective, but it has already served its purposes as our Creator, our God, the Maker of everything sent His Son, our Lord Jesus Christ to come and fulfilled the perfect sacrifice for He is fully human and sinless, He had already atone for the sins of humanity hence there’s no need for the animal sacrifice anymore. He used the analogy of underage that’s still in supervision of a parent in hope that this will help them to understand. 

Paul speak of the elemental principals which he meant the rules and traditions of the forefathers, specifically circumcisions, observances of special days and months and seasons and years, those does not matter in one’s salvation. Paul could see through the intentions of the false teachers that led them astray for they purposely exclude them so that the Galatians would long all the more to be included due to the sense of belonging that they wanted to feel. It’s only common and natural as human is made for society. Most manipulators will always use this kind of twisted or reverse psychological tactic to prey on their victims. It is despicable and it is indeed sadly common from kindergarten to high school and to workplace and family and so on. The point is that Paul understood what was happening and he was very much disturbed by the fact that the Galatians were falling into their traps and he wanted to wake them up. 

Paul then used the analogy of Hagar and Sarah in the context of making a choice purposely to choose to live in freedom that was already accomplished thru our Lord Jesus Christ on the cross and resurrected on the third day by the grace of God. It doesn’t mean to actually commit such an act of driving out a helpless woman and child. Context is important when reading and understanding the scripture. The Galatians is a letter, it is meant to be read in a whole and then capture the central theme and the issued addressed because letters always serves a purpose, what is it trying to convey? Paul was trying to push against the requirements to observe of the Mosaic law in order to be saved, that’s false, the truth is salvation by grace thru Christ.
