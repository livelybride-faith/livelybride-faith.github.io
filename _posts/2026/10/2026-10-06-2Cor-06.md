title: 2 Cor 6 - Walk in righteousness for we are the temple of the living God

---
Paul continue to elaborate on the grace of God, though he emphasized on the salvation and reconciliation of men towards God, he also added so that people do not abuse the grace received from God in vain. He then listed out how his ministry and his coworkers had suffered in hardships but still responded in righteousness. He did not shun from the truth but still delivered the speeches of truth in love. 

One of the traits that many preachers ought to learn from Paul is truthful speech not in a way that demean others but to open the eyes of people so that they focus on the truth without any downplaying but by presenting it clearly, boldly, confidently but above all by examples of a holy walk of life. In short, walking the talk. Why does preacher downplay or try to present it in a way so that people may accept it? The truth should not be compromised for the sake of avoiding hurting people’s ego or feelings, this in fact do more harm than good because the enabling actually deceive them and led them to think that there’s nothing wrong and where there’s nothing wrong there’s nothing to correct and so the lawlessness keeps on happening. 

If the truth is presented clearly without compromise then the ruler or the standard is clear, the goal is also clear, then people that listen will know that they are from the truth which yes may convict their hearts or even feel guilty about it but by then they will have known that they were wrong in their actions. When they acknowledge the mistake then they can move to the next steps towards repentance, reconciliation then redemption. This was what Paul meant by not becoming an obstacle in anyone’s way. Living the righteous life in every facets of their walk of life, not just be an angel in the church but like a devil at home or work. Do not be deceive for hypocrisy or performative behaviors are exactly the children of satan. Paul understood this so even when he is in deep suffering, he did not despair because he know that eternal heaven is real and God is real and that there’s an after, after the death of flesh. 

He did not stop at the afterlife, but he urged and exhorted the Corinthians to walk in truth and righteousness in their current life in the physical body on earth. For now after they have accepted and believe in our Lord Jesus Christ, they were the temple of the living God. The actual temple is upon those who believe, not in a physical synagogue, cathedrals, church buildings or any physical place on earth but in the believers. That is where God is living now. So do not be deceived and continue in the belief of idolators or unbelievers but worship God in spirit and in truth. 

May our Lord Jesus Christ open our eyes that we may see and help those who are blinded according to His will to also see clearly that we can worship Him and live in holiness perfect in the fear of God. May our Lord Jesus Christ help us all as leaders or preachers of His word to live a righteous life as an example to walk the talk and not just by mere speech.
