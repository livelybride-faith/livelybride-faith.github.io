As Paul’s authority was questioned by the church of Corinth, he explained to them that he had no need of letter of recommendation as the church of Corinth themselves were the evidence and proof of the effectiveness of his apostolic position and status in Christ. It was by the work and power of the Holy Spirit that enabled him to be able to be successful in founding a church, in other words planting a church. 

Paul did not argue about the education or the fact that he had years of training in the synagogue with a Rabbi but he led them to be able to think for themselves by explaining to them what it means by the passing of the old covenant and entering into a new covenant. He did this by using the metaphor or analogy of the veil from the Mosaic era which clearly meant the Old Covenant to the Jewish community. Every time they mentioned Moses or the Mosaic law, their first thought will go to the old covenant. The veil was mentioned as it’s to paint a picture for the people that the glory of the old covenant indeed was fading away but transitioning into a new glory of a new covenant in Christ that has come now. This is how Paul tried to clear their thinking, by proclaiming the gospel from another perspective, the freedom in the Spirit, but the core of the message is the same, which is Jesus Christ’s sacrifice on the cross, the salvation that leads  to freedom and eternal life in Him. The Lord, which is our Lord Jesus Christ, is the Spirit. Remember before Jesus Christ ascended, He told the disciples that He will send the Holy Spirit. That was why Paul wanted to teach them how to think as he knew that they were blinded, he wanted to set them free with the truth. 

May our Lord Jesus Christ continue to help us all to be able to truly be free in His Spirit and no longer just focus on the matters of rules and traditions but also to know the heart of God and walk in His will so that we do not miss the mark in order to glorify Him.
