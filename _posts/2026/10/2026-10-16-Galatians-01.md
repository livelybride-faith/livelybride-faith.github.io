It’s important to note that how Paul started the letter with “Paul an apostle” and you can almost hear the tone of assertion of his authority and position in Christ. Just after a short greeting, he expressed his emotion of astonishment and shock toward the Galatians because they have fallen into the trap of false teachers. He was always preaching about the grace of God, freedom in Christ, to move away from being bind by the laws of traditions in Judaism that did not matter in the context of salvation. 

The more that we read into the book of Galatians we will find out that the Galatians had gone back to the ways of Judaism, such as observing feast days, laws of the food and circumcision in order to be saved. All these will nullify the grace of God which was already completed by Christ’s death on the cross and resurrected on the third day! This is a false teaching! When I was saved, I felt free as in free without any worries of need to worship in certain way or certain method like we did in idolatry, not out of disrespect, but it truly set me free without any fear anymore for not doing things in a certain way in worshipping a God. Something had changed in me, and when I went to church, the goal of my heart was so clear and fixated on one and one only, that was to know my Lord Jesus Christ. I was so intrigued and wanted to listen everything about Him, alas I fell into the temptation of following laws again in a church. 

They kept teaching about how to follow certain methods so that one can be blessed and prosperous. This is a common bait for any human being, everyone wants to be free of worry and be blessed and be prosperous. I was not exempted. For about a half of a decade, it was a living hell of always trying to be perfect with my own actions and we follow the feast of the Judaism and calendar, don’t get me wrong, I like those very much and it help me to understand the Hebrew culture so much that it put some perspective into the context of studying the bible but it certainly did not gain me any salvation. Some days it felt like wasting so much resources and emotions into that church and that whole tree of church as they have mother church, sister churches and also children churches in the whole country and in the world. Some days I felt grateful to have experienced that and finally understood what it meant to beware of false teachers, false prophets and difference between grace of God and laws. 

Paul was concern and to the extend of certain level of anger towards the issue of the false teachings happening in Galatians, he wanted to prevent them from going deeper into false teachings and hoped that with his letters, he could wake them up. The context is always this, salvation is given by God thru Christ who sacrificed for our sins on the cross and resurrected on the third day. That was done, completed, 100%, all who believed in their heart and confess with their mouth are saved. It was that simple. His authority and apostleship was even doubted because there was no common ordination in Jerusalem as he was travelling for mission trip. It does not mean that certification or ordination in churches does not matter, but the fruit of a person matters most, the actions does not lie. He was always working hard for the gospel of Christ after the encounter with Christ. 

May our Lord Jesus Christ help us all to see the truth when He revealed to us and be grateful for the grace of God as we have been saved by Christ, to help us to focus on Christ more rather than the traditions of men…
