Paul ended the letter with another warning and it’s as if this has become a common practice in many, three warnings before a more severe or drastic consequences will be given. Self reflection is needed amongst believers as sometimes we maybe blinded and if someone told us or try to hint us about our lives, it is good to pray to God and ask the Holy Spirit to reveal the truth and help us to improve and walk in His will. 

However if it’s an abusive relationship that involves gaslighting, be careful not to fall prey into guilt when it’s really not your fault. In this case, it’s better to keep a journal of what happened, so that your memory would not be altered by the gaslighting and manipulation of the perpetrator. This is in the context of abusive authority and relationship in all walks of life. This is important because a lot of sheep falls prey into spiritual abuse as they respect the leaders of churches and more often than not, many leaders fall into the temptation of the devil because of their influence over the sheep, then the whole sheepfold became blinded and follow blindly. So even though when a leader speaks as if like Paul did or those super-apostles that Paul mentioned, we must learn to pray and discern between truth and falsehood. The only way that one can discern properly is to know and familiar with the truth, the truth is in the Holy Bible, our Lord Jesus Christ is the truth. The more that one reads, study and meditate on the Holy Bible, it will be easier for one to discern between truth and falsehood, because when some false leaders or false teachers try to deceive, one will already have the foundation of truth to compare with and will know that they have given false teachings. 

Paul spent so much time and a huge portion of his letters to battle against the false teachers but in this ending of the letter, he too reminded the Corinthians to examine & test themselves as the ultimate goal of Paul is to build the church up to be able to glorify God. There’s no perfect church in the world, everybody is flawed, but if one does not repent even after a few admonitions, then we let them be because we cannot force someone to change. Paul also taught that in this case we go no contact and let God deal with the false teachers and those who keep abusing others. Love in truth and mercy but do not become an enabler, that’s participating in injustice and wickedness. 

Judge not by appearance, but judge with right judgement. John 7:24. There may be slander after exposing the false teachers or leaders no matter it’s a church or family or organization, it will always be that way when they refuse to repent genuinely, so do not despair for they will be rewarded with punishment for their wickedness sooner or later. The most important thing is that the truth is revealed and no longer walking in falsehood. However if they do apologized sincerely and genuinely, when they put in the efforts to repent and change their ways for decades, yes for a lifetime then we have gain a soul. The easiest way to know if one truly repent is the way when they try to change but because of the trust has not yet been earned, the victim will not be able to show affection naturally, if the perpetrator lashed out or try to beg for affection just because they did some love bombings in a short term duration, then you’ll know that they did not truly changed. It takes a lifetime to earn back the trust that one has broken. There’s no easy way out. 

May our Lord Jesus Christ help us all to be able to live in peace and harmony, may the God of love and peace be with us all, may the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, the love of God, and the communion of the Holy Spirit be with all of us.
