Paul called to the Galatians to restore someone who is in transgression, giving a clear perspective to how he lived his life as an example. He too did not give up on the Galatians or even the Corinthians when they were found to be betraying his teachings by accepting the false teachings. Instead he told them to imitate him and to also restore someone gently. Gentleness does not mean to avoid conflict, but to be considerate and humble, meaning unpretentious. This is in the context of correcting someone who is willing to listen to reason. There are those that behaved as a scoffer, then consider the situation if it’s wise to do so or not as sometimes, it will backfire. 

Paul understood the twisted mindset of the false teachers and so he warned them to not be deceived for there will be consequences of their actions. It still stand true to this day, where there’s a facade or delusion of thinking that donating to priests or a church will somehow offset their sins. This is a lie. Consequences always catch up. Just look at the history of King David, yes, he was anointed, talented, blessed, had close relationship with God, confessed and repented genuinely, but he too was not spared from the torment of Absalom, his own son that betrayed him and in the end killed. God does forgive and will always forgive when a person repent genuinely, but actions has consequences, words said cannot be unsaid, hurt that was caused cannot be undone, for example, if a person used to sleep around with multiple people and someone get pregnant, a child is born, how can that be undone? That’s the meaning of consequences. It is not a threat but it’s a warning for the Galatians to choose wisely. Each and every choice that one makes will lead to a certain set of events. It is vital to bear in mind of what kind of result do you want and then make that choice. There are times it may seem that there’s no choice at all especially when there’s multiple factors or constraints in life, people sometimes had no choice but to resort to a choice that inflict the least harm and so sacrifice themselves. Let this not be hopeless case of suffering for someone like that, Paul was saying, but do not give up in doing what is right though there’s no result yet. Continue to do good as an act of faith towards God not looking towards the reward of the flesh but of the Spirit. 

Paul called out how the false leaders were living their life, rule for thee and not for me, hypocrisy and exploiting others for their own benefits. Paul was not afraid of speaking the truth in the intention of wanting to save the Galatians from false teachings – Salvation thru Christ by grace of God.
