After Paul described his past and set the stage for the readers to be able to comprehend what he will be saying next, he dive straight into the issue of circumcision. He used Titus as an example as and then he described how he opposed Cephas, also known as Peter when he stop eating with the gentile after a certain people came from James. It was a non issue until that other Jews and Barnabas follow the example of Peter.

I personally do not think that Peter was trying to cause a division, Paul thought that he was afraid, I was wondering if Peter tried to kept the peace as much as possible and it will be impossible for two factions to eat together. The story was told like this until a certain people came from James, it maybe that Peter was thinking that they will not be here long and he would miss them so they eat together but they can’t eat with the gentiles because of the rituals of cleansing and food laws. Peter did not cause any commotion here but just use a passive way to avoid conflict. Well, the truth can only be truly revealed when Jesus Christ comes back again. However the context and the point that Paul tried to make is to not be a hypocrite. Paul called out on leaders who appeared to be hypocrites as he is one of the most passionate and zealous type as a preacher. His words were full of emotions and weight, powerful and forceful at times, but many people are like that when they truly believed in what they preach, I’m not saying that all are, but yes, some. That’s why many false leaders like such passionate and zealous followers, they will try to convert them and manipulate them to spread the word because of their power of persuasion or convincing. Either those are really good performers or they truly believe in what they believed. 

Paul emphasized again that a person is justified not by the works of the law but through the faith of Jesus Christ. It is not by doing the works of the law that one can be saved, but it was Christ how have completed that for us on the cross and resurrected on the third day. This is the only gospel that is the truth, salvation is by grace of God thru our Lord Jesus Christ.
