Paul urged the readers to walk in a manner worthy of the calling, with all humility and gentleness, with patience, bearing one another in love centralized in the theme of unity of the Spirit. That means all parties, cultures, Jewish and gentiles regardless of backgrounds or origins, should practice it together without partiality. People seems to have a habit of filtering when listening or reading, this is for every believers, not just gentiles but also for Jewish community to practice that. 

The false teachers insisted that Gentiles should follow their ways but Christ never force the law of sacrifices or food or circumcision on the gentiles, the only requirement is for one to confess with their mouth and believed in their heart that they believed in Jesus Christ accepting that He is our Lord, the Son of God who has come to sacrifice for our sins and resurrected on the third day. We can see clearly of how a person behave before and after the transformation in Christ as it’s listed clearly in this chapter and it’s a reminder for us believers as we are human, and we’re easily tempted but it’s not the end when we fall into temptation. The habit and diligence in studying and reading the bible help us to go back to God’s ways when we stray. It takes practice and daily intentional choices to choose to walk in Christ over and over again without giving up. Just like when one is training their body for muscles to grow, it takes effort and practice consistently and then the muscle grows stronger. Our spirit too will grow stronger the more we choose to practice speaking the truth, work hard and pay for products, be kind, tenderhearted and forgive one another, as God in Christ has forgiven us. 

To elaborate on forgiveness, it must be done as God in Christ had, how? We repented genuinely without the intention of just wanting an offset or easy way out, we are forgiven but we still need to put in the effort of becoming Christlike, which means no longer sin nor make those same mistakes of hurting someone else. As mentioned, people tend to filter what they read or listen, cherry picking verses out of context, but here Paul had always included both sides of the coin. So when an offender kept on repeating and it is known that they just want and easy way out to manipulate and abuse others, what would Jesus Christ do? He would call them out and tell them that they are forgiven and to sin no more. It’s not just one sided that only the victim should be kind or merciful to them out of love, but the offender too must also practice kindness and mercy, if they continue to abuse others, then they are not being kind nor merciful nor walking in love at all.

It’s a stark reminder for us all to practice these together with the help from God daily and consistently no matter which origin, race, country we are from, for in Christ we are all one in the Spirit living a new life worthy of God’s calling.
