title: Galatians 3 - Children of God through faith

Paul rebuked the Galatians directly saying that they have been foolish for at first starting with the Spirit thru their belief in Christ but because of the false teachers, they fall back into the covenant of law. Christ has already completed the sacrifice and as if it’s not enough that they went back to relying on the law and not on Christ, in this they nullify the grace of God, not that it can be nullify except for themselves. 

Those who rely on the works of the law are under a curse, Christ has redeemed us from the curse of the law by becoming a curse for us, He sacrificed Himself for us, so when we believed in Him, we are free from the curse and saved. The context is in the matter of salvation, law was added because of the transgressions until the offspring from Abraham would come to whom God had promised, that is Christ. Now that Chris has come, we are no longer subject to the law which was the disciplinarian for us before the faith came, for in Christ Jesus we are all children of God through faith. This is my favourite part :


> New Revised Standard Version: Updated Edition Galatians 3

> 28 There is no longer Jew or Greek; there is no longer slave or free; there is no longer male and female, for all of you are one in Christ Jesus.
> 29 And if you belong to Christ, then you are Abraham’s offspring, heirs according to the promise.

When we believed in Christ, we are all one in Christ Jesus meaning there’s no more who is more superior or more inferior, equal in status but gifted in different areas as so to build up the whole church of God. What is the church of God? The believes of Christ Jesus, that’s us, we are suppose to build up each other with our gifts and not tear each other down or trying to make others feel inferior. It was this exact root cause that tempt the believers into wanting to go back to the law, of how twisted the mindset was to think that certain Jews are more superior in status because of their origin. So Paul broke this stronghold of deception by revealing the truth that we are all child of God through faith. 

This is the truth, now that faith has come, we are no longer subject to a disciplinarian, for in Christ Jesus you are all children of God through faith.
