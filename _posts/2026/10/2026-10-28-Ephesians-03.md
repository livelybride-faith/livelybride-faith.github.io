Paul was commissioned and called by God to share the gospel of the salvation from Christ to the gentiles but it’s never meant as a division or a separate gospel. It’s the same gospel where our Lord Jesus Christ sacrificed Himself on the cross and resurrected on the third day in order that the sins of mankind can be cleansed and be reconciled with God. It’s a shock towards the Jewish community as they have followed the Mosaic law for a long time, in fact it’s understandable why they had a hard time parting with it or to move on from that. 

Keep in mind though, Paul did not said that they have to leave their whole traditions or culture but in the context of salvation, he wanted all the hearers of the gospel of Christ to fully understand the truth, that it is by grace of God that the gift of salvation is given to all mankind. It was out of envy and comparison that the striving begun to take root in the false teachers’ heart that they created a rule so that they can feel superior but most importantly it’s to use religion or the faith of believers to manipulate and control the people. The mystery that Paul spoke of is this exact truth that God has given the gift of salvation to the gentiles as well and some did not get the memo. So it is up the believers of Christ to live it out, including the suffering part. Paul understood the importance of his role in the mission to spread the gospel amongst the gentiles hence he was willing to be a servant of that to the point of risking his life and being imprisoned either in jail or house arrest. His goal was never to take sides, not the gentiles nor the Jewish community, but it was for that eternal purpose that God had carried out in Christ Jesus, our Lord.

Paul then prayed for the readers with one goal in mind so that they know the love of Christ that surpasses knowledge, so that they may be filled with all the fullness of God. His prayers was for the benefits of the readers and God Himself. He gain nothing from that prayers except that God’s will to be fulfilled. This is a selfless prayer and from a righteous person. Which I think is one of the most important prayer of all time as we can see that after centuries, that the gospel of Christ has spread to the gentiles around the world. I’m talking about those who truly love and know God, who put real effort into studying the bible, God’s words just to get to know God.
