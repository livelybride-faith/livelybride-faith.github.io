Paul stated that he must boast, in this context he meant by giving glory to God of what he went thru, which was the hardship of being persecuted and nearly killed in Damascus. He was hunted by a king who had authority and power to make him disappear but God protected him. That was the point, he was a mere nobody when it comes to comparison of a king of Damascus, what else could he do but run and hide? Even so, the king had much resources to captured a person so in contrast the protection of God was even more sure and magnified. That’s the perspective of how Paul gave glory to God for he could not depend on himself or anyone for his own safety. 

As the Corinthians were baited by the super apostles of dreams and visions or revelations, Paul stated that he too knew of someone that was caught up into third heaven and paradise. From the way that he described it, it seems like he was talking about himself yet he did not want to emphasized or talk much of it for first, no mortal was permitted to repeat it, secondly he wanted to avoid glamour and to let others see him as it is, the truth. There are times that a person desire attention and audience hence sometimes people may be forgetful and became fixated of the things that does not really matter much when it comes to more important things. Mundaneness in life could tempt someone into snares and traps of thinking that if they had been thru this then they must have been really chosen by God because no one else had that. Charm and bait are the same thing, it was always the natural law of predator and prey in the world. The only way to keep from it is to rely solely on God, not men, not self, not any creation at all. 

Paul was an intelligent person, but he did not use this against the Corinthians to deceive them. He use it so that he could build them up. For anyone that read the letters of Paul, it may feel like he covered many ground of arguments like a super sharp lawyer by leading the thoughts of the readers with his words and some may feel trapped or no way to argue back reasonably. Some will feel threatened or trapped and dislike his methods, but some may begin to understand his true intention is to help remove the veil so that they can see the truth. 

Paul knew the people of the Corinthians were not perfect but he also loved them, so he was trying to find the best way that could really be beneficial to them. Some churches or audience can benefit from the visiting of such a Paul but some may benefit even more from a faraway hearty letters. In order to build someone up, there are many ways and Paul chose the method wisely. He removed as many threats as possible so that the recipients can receive the truth not because he wanted to bait them into trusting him blindly. He just want them to be able to discern between truth and false teachings. 

May our Lord Jesus Christ help us all to be able to discern between truth and falsehood, to lead us away from temptation and to help us to build others up the way that God wills.
