Paul wrote this part to prepare the heart of those who were eager to participate in the provision towards his ministry. As a leader of a ministry or church, his concern about whether or not they would get the promised provision, if not he would have to find another way before it’s too late. Although he was concern about that, still he tried to encourage the Corinthians to keep their promises as he already told the Macedonians about the gift. 

It is unclear what was the arrangement but mostly had to do with the needs of the saints which means to the poor in the Christian society. History showed that Christians had a really tough time during that time. It maybe that those who converted to Christianity would have lost their source of income and social circle. They would have to depend solely on God’s provisions thru Paul or other Christians. This isn’t about merely church planting or spreading the word of God, in that situation it was about survival, they depended on the donations to survive. 

Paul emphasized one thing, he stated clearly, the point is this, that one should give after they have made up their mind, not regretfully or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver. So if one is not ready to give or feels that there are other obligations that they need to fulfill first, such as those involving family members, do the duty first else it will lead to bitterness. There are examples of how when one was under the influence of an emotional moment that they made a compulsive decision then it led to another worse situations within their lives and then they shift the whole blame to the giving itself. This is in the context of knowing that there was already a need in the family or in their lives, not some secret cover up debts that they didn’t know of. Just imagine this, if one shuns away from providing bread to the family but kept on giving to the needy, while their family members beg in the street for food, how can that be sensible? So Paul was asking them to be sensible in their giving but at the same time encouraged those who are able to give generously for God will not shortchange them. 

When I was in a terrible needy situation, I prayed and begged hard to God for provision, deliverance or healing, and when the prayers were answered, there was an overflowing of thanksgiving towards God and the people who were involved. That’s just a natural response, it humbles and pure without any mask nor performative intention. It was pure gratefulness, awe and love towards God. On the other hand, during the time of abundance, giving generously towards a godly cause without any regrets also produces a sense of belonging to God because that’s what God’s children do, the participation in partnership with our God will help to feel that we are part of Him which in turn produces a sense of joy and satisfaction. 

Have you ever notice why somedays when there arise a chance that we can help somebody out of the mundane schedule, even just a little gesture such as giving way to the elders or people who are in need, or helping out when they met with some accidents, then we feel good and joyful? Then when we see something like that happen, we feel a glimpse of faith in humanity restored? Hopeful? That’s what Paul meant, not rejoicing over others’ calamities but able to participate in godly cause, that we truly belong to Christ.

May our Lord Jesus Christ help us all to experience His provision and bless us so that we give sensibly, generously and cheerfully without any regrets.
