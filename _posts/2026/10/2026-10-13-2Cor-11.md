Paul was clearly back to his tone of firmness and to persuade the Corinthians to see the truth and he spoke of the divine jealousy for the church just like how God was jealous for the Israelites, in using this familiar language, he actually helped to remind the readers to understand that the false teachers were trying to deceive them and their thoughts led astray from a sincere and pure devotion to Christ. 

Only those who truly loved will be able to have such care, only with such true concern can only one react in such a way. For someone who doesn’t care will just walk away and let them be. This always shows in a chronic broken relationship where one kept paddling an ore in a rowing boat instead of two, they’d be going round and round in circle. Unless there’s two ways response and effort, the one how kept on giving and trying will walk away sooner or later because there’s no point in trying. Paul cared, a lot, to the point that he had to spent his emotional energy, feelings, time and finance towards the Corinthians. Building requires a lot of sacrifice, he did that and on the expense of Macedonians too. 

Just imagine how would you feel if you as a parent had been giving and sacrificing for the sake of the wellbeing of your children, and on their teenage years screaming at you that you don’t love them just because you told them the truth of how someone is deceiving them? Don’t get me wrong, it is perfectly understandable because of the fear of missing out, everybody wants to belong, so teenagers are at that point of phase, more patient and understanding and more communication are actually needed than just saying “Because I said so”. It’s better to point out the consequences of both actions, like how we weight out pros and cons so that they learn how to think. Then let them decide and learn from their mistakes. Of course this is in a controlled manner, not involving anything life or death nor drugs or violence that may harm self or others. 

Another point is that Paul stood up against these false teachers who were abusing or manipulating the Corinthians which they chose to put up with. It’s really heartbreaking sometimes when those who were abused chose to go against or “stood up” against those who really loved them and spoke the truth but put up with the abuser. This in turn only enable the abuser much more, however some situations were due to conditioning or grooming involved in their development years and hence it should be handled cautiously when it comes to judgement or counsel for abusive relationship. The context here though with Paul and the Corinthians is how they were willing to accept the false teachings but reject the truth that comes from God. He told them clearly that Satan disguises himself as an angel of light, and naturally his ministers also disguises themselves as ministers of righteousness. However he knew that their end will match their deeds, consequences always caught up. There’s no hiding nor escape. 

Then Paul reminded them if they had wanted to follow those false teachers just because of their positions, origin of birth, their efforts or success in their ministry, Paul did not lack any of those, in fact, even more so than of all those so called super apostles. In saying all these, he was not trying to compare with the false teachers but he want to bring out that he was not less and in doing that he glorified God because all those false teachers did that for their own selfish benefits. Paul did all that because he believed in Christ and love God. 

May our Lord Jesus Christ help us all always take notice of how He’s speaking to us from time to time, thru people or environment or in some incidents so that we do not stray away from the truth and be deceived. May God bless us all with the spirit of truth and revelation, to help us walk in righteousness.
