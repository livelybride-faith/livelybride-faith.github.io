Paul continued to lay out the contrasts and differences of how a non Christian and a Christian should behave or practice in their lives. Ephesians seems to have the same exact problem that we are not facing all over the world whether it’s online or in real physical life, we see people, both believers and non believers getting involved in sexual immorality, bound in all types of addictions, struggling in greed or due to FOMO. This is the mass effect of failure to comprehend the truth that comes after salvation from Christ. 

A confusion seems to plague the Christian community led to generations of thinking that salvation by grace means that they can continue to sin or harm others, this is wrong as this is categorized as abusing the grace of God. No one can deceive God, consequences always catch up. This is the exact pattern that comes from pagan idolaters, worshiping and giving offerings in fear with the hope that the consequences or their guilt can be offset or washed off. Our Lord Jesus Christ was very clear in His teaching, “your sins are forgiven, go and sin no more”. Forgiveness from God is an act of grace thru our Lord Jesus Christ on the cross who then resurrected on the third day, that is in the context of salvation. After that, true believers learn about God’s ways and believe truly in their hearts that this is the way, the truth and the life, when the mind is transformed, the behaviours and actions follow. 

This is real as I was an idolater as well and was numb to all those vulgar talk, sexual immorality, greed and never think that addiction was a problem. However after I accepted our Lord Jesus Christ as my Lord and Saviour, my mind began to change but it wasn’t without intention or effort, there’s a zeal that overcame my heart to want to know more about our Lord Jesus Christ and so my actions followed. Just pure zeal to want to read the bible, worship God, sing to God, feeling grateful for what He has done, perspective and mindset changed first then the choices and actions followed. A pure intention to do everything and just to live for God, then everything worldly that attracted me before seems so dull and despicable that I just want to go home to God. The longing to be with God can sometimes be so overwhelming due to realization of all these in the world passes away and sometimes it can’t be help but just to long more our Lord Jesus Christ to come again quickly, immediately. That feeling of I want, becomes “I want God” and that helped to understand a little bit more of Psalms 23. 

Paul was right, before accepting Christ, I was asleep, dead; then after accepted Jesus Christ, I woke up, this is in the context of really knowing the value of life that’s given by God and to live a life worthy of His grace and mercy. Not in the way that He is strict or dictatorial, in fact, in opposite, of how patient and loving He is to wait for me to be ready and to be transformed by the renewing of my mind. When I’m ready, He was and is always ready for me and to lead me closer to Him. He is so gentle and compassionate that when my pain in my heart or trauma was too much to bear, in my begging and crying, suddenly there was warmth and comfort with light and joy filled my heart, that’s how I know it’s Him. One without God may try to use substances to dull their pain, but it is possible to move away from that and to be filled with the Holy Spirit. 

Then Paul spoke of how a marriage should work within Christ, wives, subject to their husbands but at the same time, husbands should love their wives as they love themselves. Each of them, love the wives, and a wife respect her husband. No striving, no competition nor contempt, then only there can be peace and harmony in the marriage. Not easy but simple. May our Lord Jesus Christ help us all to be able to live a life worthy of God’s gift and grace.
