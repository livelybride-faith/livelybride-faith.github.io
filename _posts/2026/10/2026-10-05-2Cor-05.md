title: 2 Cor 5 - Assurance of eternal home in God thru Christ
tag: 2Cor
---
Paul gave a lively illustration regarding how one has the Spirit of God, that is when a believer is filled with the Holy Spirit, one of the symptoms but not confined to that only one nor does it mean that if a believer does not experience, they do not have the Holy Spirit. Paul’s way of describing it was my own experience, that this has set me free from lies and deception thinking that there was something wrong with me. 

For years, it felt like it’s meaningless to live on earth not to the point that want to self-annihilate but just feel so pointless and has this deep longing to go back home to God. The earth has never felt like home, yes there were times that it can be said that it seems like it felt like home when experiencing warmth or favor or the goodness and peace around. Those are just like virtual glimpses where the brain and chemicals of the body can’t really tell whether it’s a fact or placebo as the emotions were overwhelmed. Emotions are link to the chemicals release in the body hence it will feel real. However when it all dies down, there’s only that emptiness again, then that deep longing to be with God truly face to face in reality not just confined in this physical body. For years, I never understood naked dreams even when all is well, but Paul’s wordings in this chapter help me to understand that is some deep longing to be further clothed with our heavenly dwelling. That groaning and burden described by Paul felt like light in the dark as the meaning of those dreams becomes clear. 

This become another anchor of hope that one can hold on to, because we know that we are going somewhere where God has prepared for us, eternal in heaven. Because of that, in this time on earth, it makes everything even more sense for a believer to please God in life as one day we must all appear before the judgement seat of Christ and all will receive due recompense for actions done in the body whether good or evil. How hopeful is that knowing that those who have wronged us and we wanting justice now but surrendering vengeance to God which cause so much struggles of pain and agony in the heart, that one day they will be judge. This is in the context of those who have been wronged but never get the consolation of justice being served, it is not for self-righteousness to linger nor to grow. 

Hence this leads to the fear of the Lord, a reverence and knowing that it will all happen soon, so Paul encouraged the church in Corinth to be mindful as to live for Christ. He reminded them the gospel of how Christ died for all so that all live, no longer for self but for Christ and in doing so become Christlike for we have been reconciled to God. It is a second chance for all who have been dead because of sin and now considered as righteous because of Christ before God, that we should live that way worthy of Christ. 
May our Lord Jesus Christ help us all to live for Him and console us in our spirit and soul when our hearts grieve and groan from the burden of the longing to be with Him.
