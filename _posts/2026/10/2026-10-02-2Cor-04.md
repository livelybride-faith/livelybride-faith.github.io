Although Paul experienced much hardship and tribulations in the matter of the rejection at church of Corinth, starting with grieving emotions in the letter, he did not stop there. In chapter four, he demonstrated that his hope was not crushed because he put his faith in Christ. He knew very well that it was God’s mercy that he can minister to people and to the Lord, not because he earned that, so he felt grateful even though it seem like it’s taken away from him. He did not feel entitled hence he was able to let go. 

That is a great reminder and consolation for us who have lost much in various tribulations and sufferings, when we are able to align our heart with God and the truth again, then our burdened heart pulled down by the weight of not wanting to let go maybe because we have forgotten and started to feel entitled to something or someone or some favor from God, will be at peace for now we see clearly the truth that we are not entitled. Paul did not feel entitled to the church of Corinth but in fact people know that he had the rights because he founded the church, instead he demonstrated humility and willingness to yield for the sake of peace and harmony in the community back then. 

Paul’s faith indeed rest in God and this encourages us believers now in this time, to put our trust in God, naturally then people shall see that Christ is visible in us for we will not react nor response based on the circumstances but according to God’s will and traits. This is how we know that we are the children of God. The actions match what we believe in, so as Paul and the apostles spoke as what they believe in and how Paul also demonstrated in his actions and walk of life in the journey of faith. 

May our Lord Jesus Christ help us all to align our hearts and mind back to His will and strengthened us to be able to let go and surrender to Christ, putting our trust in Him and grow our faith in Him.
