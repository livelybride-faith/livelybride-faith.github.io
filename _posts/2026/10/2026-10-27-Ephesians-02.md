title: Ephesians 2 - Saved by Grace, the gift of God
tag: Ephesians
---
Paul spoke of a typical transition of a new believer of Christ who sinned and followed whatever that the world deemed right but actually sinning against God, following only by the feelings and passion of the flesh mostly in indulgences of to satisfy the fleshly desires such as immorality in sexual interactions, adultery, idolatry, stealing to fulfill self benefits, deceptive and wicked in conducts and behaviours for various reasons. All of these are the opposites of the godly way which Christ taught us to be righteous. 

Each person has sinned and no one could save themselves except through faith which is the gift of God. No one can boast about this at all because it was Christ who fulfilled the sacrifice on the cross for the sins of mankind then He resurrected on the third day. It’s not old news repeating on a broken record but it’s a the truth and the gospel of salvation that was freely given to us and for us to freely spread it to whomever that needs it. The Ephesians like most of us were gentiles meaning the uncircumcised, specifically not a Jewish nor an Israelite; supposedly estranged from the covenants of the promise, without hope and without God in the world. It was our Lord Jesus Christ that reconciled us gentiles and remove the hostility between us and God. By that, we gentiles become members of the household of God built on the foundation of the apostles and prophets with Christ Jesus Himself as the cornerstone. This is to say that we no longer need to follow the rules of the traditions to be circumcised physically to be saved. This is a fundamental truth as Christ has fulfilled it by grace of God. There’s nothing that we can do to save ourselves. Our responsibility is to live a life worthy of His gift. 

Imagine that parents sacrificed themselves for their kids to study and get a good education to help them in their lives, when they became successful, they cannot denied that it was a gift and say that it was all their hard work, they were given a chance by the gift of education by grace. They can only then live a life, to work diligently in their study to get good grades so that they become worthy of the sponsorship. It was already given as a gift freely. The hard work comes after the gift as a thanksgiving. Just like how Christians suffered in faith for the future that’s to come. 

As for the temple, we are now in Christ and Christ is in us and we are the temple of the Holy Spirit which God dwell in us and there’s no longer a need for a physical building such as the ark of the covenant for God to live in, not that God can be constraint in a space. We as Christians are now the assembly of God that is joined together and to grow together as a holy temple. 
May our Lord Jesus Christ help us to live a life worthy of His gift of salvation for us and help us to build up each other in love.
