Paul specifically told the Galatians and the readers of the letters that circumcision itself benefit nothing at all. This was due to the false teachings from the sect of Jewish community that roots out from Judaism, it doesn’t mean that Paul rejected them from being a Christian but they were the ones that rejected Christ. 

The law of Moses was valid but because Christ has come and fulfilled the sacrifice that were needed to be restore or reconcile humanity with God, it has served it’s purpose, for example, a used dial up phone and a smart phone, can we say that the used dial up phone was useless or wrong? No, it has already done it’s job. Or cassettes, CDs and digital streaming platforms.  So this argument that Paul was trying to make is not to demean Judaism but because after we are saved by Christ, then it is a false teaching to go back to want to be saved by the law. 

So, does it mean that we don’t have to follow any rules since we are in freedom? We can do whatever we want? No! In fact, Paul said, the only thing that counts is faith working through love. Love is patient, love is kind, love does not envy the whole nine yards. Salvation is already done by Christ for us, however we must not use our freedom to do whatever we want however we want in self indulgence but to serve one another in love. This is in face very much real in reality, treating others badly will result in negative consequences, it always comes back. Of course, it doesn’t mean that we treat others kindly it will come back to us kindly, but Peter said “But even if you do suffer for doing what is right, you are blessed”. 

Paul urged the Galatians and for today, us, believers of Christ, to live by the Spirit and to not get involve in sexual immorality, impurity, debauchery, idolatry, sorcery, enmities, strife, jealousy, anger, quarrels, dissensions, factions, envy, drunkenness, carousing, and things like these. We can discern whether one has the fruit of the spirit base on this :“love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, generosity, faithfulness, gentleness, and self-control”. 

When Paul called out the wickedness, he did not stop there, he led the thoughts of the readers back to a solution or a goal in mind. He knew the root of the whole false teachings was because of envy and when envy comes, strive comes too. May our Lord Jesus Christ guide us by the Spirit and help us to live by the Spirit.
