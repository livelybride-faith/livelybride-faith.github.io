After Paul has written of his suffering and anguish of the rejection of the church of Corinth, he did not stop there but he switch to the direction of those who repent because of his previous letter. This is also one of the trait that we ought to learn from, Paul was able to find joy in affliction and chose to shift his perspective even when the circumstances did not change. He did not focus on the bad situations only but to see the good in the bad. 

In a lifetime, most often than not, people tend to focus or fixated on the evil and wicked people which we really shouldn’t because why do they deserve any attention at all? Alas, that’s just how human respond, no one is exempted. In fact, we should be mindful of what we choose to focus on, that is in righteousness, not only to show favor or be in fellowship with the righteous but to commend them and encourage them to continue to walk in righteousness. There are some leaders or believers who will always demean believers’ zeal for the Lord and may even cause them to no longer show their zeal towards the Lord just because of the way that it was shown or that it was too passionate, or that it was too emotional, the point is that the leaders want their followers to worship God the way that they wanted, that is very sad; what about God’s way? Why can’t a believer or the child of God be free to worship God the way that is truthful in their emotional expression? Some even told the believers that in prayers you should do this or should not do that, for God’s sake it’s a conversation with God. It’s suppose to be private in communion with God. If we read the psalms, how strong those words that they have recorded in the bible?! Those are like their diaries, journals entries that were private towards God. We get to read it and it is a blessing to us because then no one could tell us it is wrong to pray this way or that way. My dear brothers and sisters, just talk to God, He can handle us because He created us. We don’t have to downplay anything, just worship Him in spirit and in truth. This is why I prefer worshipping God in private for I admit that I’m susceptible to the accusations of believers around me directing me on how I should or should not worship Him. How despicable… 

What does that has to do with this chapter? It’s the way that how Paul was so direct and without any downplay of his zeal for God and it spilled out towards the church of Corinth because of how he felt towards God and it naturally flow into what belongs to God. He cared so much about God and so he cared much also about the church of God. This is ownership. A leader must always be in the mindset of ownership and responsibility. Hence he did not give up easily, and he chose to also take notice of those who repented because of his letters. Now back to the worshipping God with such zeal and passion, there was not only bad but also those kind of worship inspired many others to freely worship God as they wanted to. That was exactly why some leaders could not take it, then we must ask are those leaders truly the disciple of Christ? Why are they jealous of the believers worshipping God? The real question is why are they jealous of God? Sound’s familiar? That’s the devil’s trait. 

May God help us all to repent while there are still time. It’s the similar issue in the church of Corinth, they were arguing and debating causing division because of how one should worship God. So, Paul continued to encouraged those who accepted his teachings and those who repent by stating clearly how he was comforted and consoled even though in grief because they were saved by their own repentance. That’s how much Paul loved the souls and how he wanted people to have a clear truthful view of God. 

May God help us all to be able to discern what is that good and perfect will of God so that we can worship Him the way that He created us to worship Him and not be dictated by others.
