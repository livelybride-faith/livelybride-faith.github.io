Paul continued to explain in his letter, a gentle reminder, this whole book is a letter, and it was meant to be read in one sitting just like any letter but it could also be processed over time. It’s like sometimes people tried to explain something thru limited words and it wasn’t until a time that one finally understood the true meaning. It took me a long time to understand what Paul is trying to say as well, only thru grace of God and by the works of Holy Spirit that I start to understand a bit more as God reveal it. The initiation though is from ourselves to pray for understanding before we read the bible and ask God to give us the spirit of wisdom and revelation so that we may know Him. 

Now back to the argument that Paul tried to stir the readers to think, he was trying to lead their thoughts by not telling them what to think but how to think, notice how he bring them to one point of law to grace and then grace to faith and now about Abraham. All Jewish and even gentiles of the faith knows about Abraham, hence Paul tried to lead them to truth by also using the scriptures, that is the Old Testament. If one grasp it, which I personally believe is one of the turning point in one’s thought or thinking pattern is to introduce a question or to give room for some logic behind the whole chain of events. In this example, one sentence will act like hammer to break that stronghold which is 

> 10 How then was it reckoned to him? Was it before or after he had been circumcised? It was not after but before he was circumcised. 
> 11 He received the sign of circumcision as a seal of the righteousness that he had by faiths while he was still uncircumcised. 

The promise that God made to Abraham was before he was circumcised, hence righteousness was reckoned to him by faith. 
The opposite of faith is a religious spirit. Just look at all religion, everything has to do with rituals and rules or methods of offering. Again I say this not out of disrespect for I myself was a very religious person in idolatry from buddhism to hinduism to taoism or whatever superstitious acts. It brought me nothing but terror and fear, deceptions and blindness. Told me what to think and not how to think. Nothing ever made sense until our Lord Jesus Christ revealed Himself to me. I was saved because He revealed Himself to me. Not because I went to church, not because I read the bible, not because I saved someone, but during my lowest time, before I even touched a proper bible, mormon’s don’t count for they contain deceptive and twisted scriptures. It pains me that some are still in deception because they were brainwashed and blinded by their religious leaders. Though I do not deny their diligence in evangelism. 

My salvation has nothing to do with religion, it was all faith thru the grace of God. Then I went to church purely to know everything about Jesus Christ. However it’s so easy even to get lost in a community of believers of Christ, so many practices due to lies of thinking to save oneself with religious serving, rituals and superstitious belief, yes, even in church. It was like going backwards to Judaism. It cost so much time and so much resources being lost and produced so much hatred and wrath, in the end it was like that tree with only leaves but no fruit when you go near it to pluck some fruit for food. That was how I experience & finally understood the meaning of that verse. All for show and performative. 

It is not that the Old Testament’s law is not good, but if one really grasp the principle of each rules and laws, it all comes from one root that is to love others as ourselves so that there is harmony and peace in a society. As for the sacrificial and offering’s law, our Lord Jesus Christ has come and it was no longer needed for He is the perfect lamb, Who sacrificed Himself willingly on the cross and thru Him by faith in Him, accepting Him, believing in Him to be our Saviour is how we are saved. This is also a reminder for myself, that my salvation and my faith has nothing to do with my works and there’s absolutely nothing that I can boast about except when I was in my weakness, God came thru. All glory belongs to God, always has been, always will be. Without God, we are nothing. May God help us all to truly grasp the meaning of faith and grace from Him that we stay humble and be reminded that God is our Maker and Creator.
