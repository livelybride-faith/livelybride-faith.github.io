This chapter is for those who love a good rule book that can be followed or reminded from time to time as a follower of Christ. Some people like to ask if there’s some rules to follow in a sense that they are able to set a goal or focus on these rules so that they do not stray away and it’s not a bad idea to always be reminded of these things. 

The human mind tends to distorts reality even for the sanest person in the world, memories are unreliable for facts, any detective will be able to affirm that. However Paul did not want this to be just like any rulebook or commandments like a chain of religious’ rules, he wants the reader to learn to discern what is the will of God and to think with sober judgement. In short, he wants the reader to learn “how to think” and not “what to think”. Critical thinking is important for judgement but often time it’s being used wrongly to judge others with a log in our eyes. This is what we need to improve and ask God for help to let us judge with righteous judgement on the basis of God’s mercy. It is by mercy and grace of God that we receive salvation and it is vital that we always remember that lest we become entitled and arrogant by the delusions of we earned it. Many people hated Christians because of how Christians behave, entitled, arrogant, making others feel less worthy than them, this is how Christians get the quote of “holier than thou”. Although not all fall into the same category but most did that and cause this quote to be infamous among Christians. 

Paul address the core of the issues of arrogance and pride rooted out of insecurity and striving by teaching the readers back then that all Christians are one body of Christ, each person is a member of a body and each person has their own function. He was speaking in human terms as well due to the limitation of words and for the lack of a better word in human language. The body is a metaphor but Paul made it clear that different functions means different gifts such as prophecy, ministering, teaching, encouraging, giving, leading and compassion. Not everyone will have all gifts or more than one gift, but each one will have at least some or one of a gift. This is to bring out the importance of harmony in a church, the body of Christ. Many people likes to compare because maybe they were brought up by comparison from their parents which were also brought up by comparison from their parents and so on. Generational trauma often times need intentional breaking and that takes time but it is not impossible. I’d like to think that Paul understood to some extend about generational trauma and consequences from upbringing or circumstances that’s out of one’s control. He did not focus on those too much but he gave solutions, steps, and action plan, he’s one of those man that look forward and desire walking more than talking but he also did talk a lot. So he gave a whole list of how to behave or rather the goal of a Christian’s life. Seriously, just read from v9-21, I have a feeling that people will find that it seems quite impossible in this era of evil in the world because we desire justice. Again it is not wrong to desire justice, and Paul did not say to avoid justice or judgement but it is the standard of God to be that perfect. 

For example, do not overcome evil by evil, meaning that if someone hit us, we do not hit back but overcome evil with good, we call the authorities and let them get apprehended for their actions. If a wife is being abused by a husband, the wife must overcome it by involving the authorities for accountability on the husband since they won’t stop the abuse. However often times, abusive relationship involves years of condition towards the human mind and emotion, so they became accustomed and fear took hold of the victims, so they will need social and financial support from others to help them to take action. It doesn’t mean that one should give them money, yes maybe some in short term, but the goal is to help to rebuild their lives and let them learn a skill to be able to support themselves financially. So that in the long term, they will be able to stand on their feet and be a human being again. 
This is to encourage us Christians that if our Lord has put us somewhere in this kind of situation that we see someone being abused, then be wise and also get advice from others or authorities that are experienced in these then plan how to help the victims but only if they want help. Most will resist at first, so what can be done is to pray for them and be ready when they finally comes to us for help. Living in harmony does not mean being silence when injustice happened, but to overcome evil with good, speak up, first to the person itself, if they refuse to stop then gather two or three brothers and sisters in Christ to speak with them. If they still do not stop, then they shall be excommunicated. 

This is in the context of intentional adultery, idolatry, deception, wickedness, evil, violence, injustice that hurt people. Showing kindness to them is not going to the authorities on first warning or giving them chances to change if they show any signs of repentance & regrets, that they are willing to follow your action plan and advice, counselling or therapy. That’s what it meant. It is not by showing unconditional unlimited grace and mercy by allowing them to hurt others indefinitely. That’s just cruelty to the victims and not hearing the cries of the victim will in turn cause our cries not to be heard by God. Why then do you think this world is so evil? Maybe somehow it’s because we’ve stop being kind and just by closing our eyes and ears to the weak and needy which then allow the perpetrators to run wild and free without immediate consequences. 

Prison or jail is not revenge, valid law punishment is not revenge, it is allowing God to do the vengeance as the perpetrator are still alive to have the chance to repent. Revenge is when someone take matters into their own hands and then the consequences are lives were lost. Leaving room for the wrath of God is not a passive waiting for God to take action but to speak up, to advice, counsel, provide guidance, and if they listen, then you’ve won their soul for God. May God help us all to live a life worthy of Him and to live peaceably in harmony as much as we can in our society and help us to not be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good.
