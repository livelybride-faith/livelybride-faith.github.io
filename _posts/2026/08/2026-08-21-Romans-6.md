In this chapter, Paul knew how the people were thinking, twisting and turning to find a loophole in law, this is what happens when people are bound by many laws without understanding the principles. I was one of them too before I study the bible personally, seriously then God started to reveal the truth. I had so many questions that I annoyed pastors and teachers to the point that I was labeled as troublesome. That was after they told us what can be done, what cannot be done, in simple words, they brainwashed us to the point of what to think, and not how to think. They were suppose to teach principles but they taught rules and laws of the unverifiable, taking scriptures out of context in order to have control over others. This caused much loss, anger and rage for those who were involved and they wonder why people leave. 

There are consequences because kids will grow up, people will wake up, the blind will see again and what happens when they do? What are they suppose to do with those rage and anger? Why was it that they were the one caused it and the victim had to bear the suffering of struggling with the rage and hatred? And so we pray for God to avenge us for vengeance belongs to God. How else then the involved victims can get vindication? Does justice not apply to pastors or leaders of church? Church is the body, Christ is the head. Do you think that the head does not know what the body is doing? So when they continue to blind others they too are blinded. So think about the consequences when we interact with people. Deliberately provoking others or manipulate others has lead to nothing but war and hatred. The loss is always much more than the gain. 

What does this mean to us then as reader of the book of Romans now? As some believers kept abusing the grace of God thinking that it will increase the glory of God, Paul said no. Continuing to sin will lead to death, for the wages of sin is death. The context here is not about salvation but it’s about post-salvation, after we believed and accepted Jesus Christ as our Lord and Saviour, we died to our sins and we follow Jesus Christ to live a holy life, there is still struggle but that’s why we rely on God. 

Paul had to use obedience as the word in human term because of the limitation of our limitations. Paul was speaking to scoffers as scoffers always do this, “you said grace, right? then I’ll max it and see how you react” “but you were the one that said sin leads to death! then i must not eat the pork!”. These two extremes were done because of sarcasm to inflict pain or control over those who tried to teach righteously, scoffers often use sarcasm in their reaction to something. I personally believe that this is the hardest struggle one can face for I myself was that very same scoffer that was so blinded, until I study the bible and the Holy Spirit reveal it layer by layer to me. This is important because I was no longer a passive listener to preachers or pastors or teachers but to put effort and time, praying and seeking the truth when studying the bible. It is the truth that set me free. 

So Paul wanted to bring the audience back to a central point to the truth that sin leads to death, but the free gift of God, GRACE; is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord. Gifts are for us to cherish and not to be boasted on the position of the receiver but of the Giver as it is not earned. In our culture, often times we received gifts from parents or others thinking that we must have done something good towards them that we received a much more expensive gift. Gift exchange culture has also blurred the line and lead people to be confuse about the meaning of gift. So many will think that they earn the gift. Not so in this case of salvation. We were sinners when Christ died for us.
