The island that Paul and the whole ship crew and prisoners landed on was called Malta. Paul performed miracles, healings & was not affected even when a viper seized his hand, it is unsure whether he was bitten or not but the point is that he was unharmed and protected by God. The residents in the island thought of him as god and entertained them hospitably. They honor Paul by providing all that Paul and the ship crew needs when they were about to set sail. 

As Paul did not do anything that was worthy of being imprisoned nor death in regards against the Roman empire’s law, so he had the freedom to live by himself with a guarding soldier, this allowed him to have the opportunities to meet with people but especially the local leaders of the Jews so that he can preach about the gospel of Christ to them. He testified about the kingdom of God and taught them about Jesus Christ from the law of Moses and from the prophets. Basically they studied the Old Testament together, though some were convinced and believed, some did not. So Paul quoted that the Holy Spirit was right in saying to your ancestors through the prophet Isaiah

There are times when we tried to correct people’s mindset in hope that they may see the truth but some just refused and hang on stubbornly upon deception. Some maybe caused by decades of brainwashing or some just refused to truly study the bible and seek God’s truth. Some just chose to follow what others believe and does not do their own research and so foolishly fall into the trap of the devil’s deception. Some still twist the scriptures to fit their own perception leading others to the fall into the same pit. Woe to them for causing the little ones to fall into pits. 

Here Paul did not outstay his welcome in Rome, two years he lived at his own expense. He continue to proclaimed the kingdom of God and teach about our Lord Jesus Christ. I imagined this is one of those moments that sometimes certain scriptures cannot be explain merely by words but one has to live it, to experience it and then the understanding dawn on them “so this is what it meant”. So what happens when we experience this that some people are so dull hearted? We shake the dust off our feet and continue to proclaim the gospel of Christ to others. Just like Paul did, he went to the Gentiles after the whole ordeal with the synagogues. 

For decades, this too happened in different denominations in Christendom, some believed in deceptive lies about doctrines and different beliefs, some tried hard to proclaim the truth, some may perceived and blessed are they who accepted and repent but some just continue to believe the lies they were told for fear of loss. Sometimes we have the chance to correct it, we thank God when people can repent, believe and change their ways, but if not, let us not despair and continue to proclaim the truth and lead others to truth, most importantly to lead people to Christ. May our Lord Jesus Christ help us all to be bold in completing this mission.
