Paul was brilliant in addressing both the Jewish and Gentile community in his time, imagine how the letters were being read in synagogues and gatherings of Christian at their time. Everyone would be silent trying to listen to what Paul has to say. First, he addressed the wrong teachings, and then he anticipated that the audience would overreact and over-correct to become hostile against the Jewish community. So then he addressed the Gentiles, he was actually disarming the great division among the Jewish and Gentiles because they were so fixated in who is right and who is wrong. Paul stood on the foundation of the truth and that was why he was able to see both sides and knew what they were thinking about. I can’t help but to see the footsteps and traces of our Lord Jesus Christ just like when He was teaching His disciples and the Pharisees. 

In this chapter, Paul emphasized on the ELECTION BY GOD, that is Israel, which He promised to Abraham which was to bring out the trustworthiness of God. If it wasn’t for Israel then there would be no salvation, but God was not unjust or impartial, when Israel was disobedient, they too receive the consequences, and if they, the elect, was not spared from that, so too all others shall not be spared from the consequences of disobedience. The Gentiles may have boasted against the Jewish community thinking that they now have Christ and the salvation or maybe showing some contempt, or that they are better than the Jewish community, in truth, both were still in a competition or striving against each other. How can one strive or try to boast who is better when it’s a gift? There’s no logic in that at all as it’s a gift?! All have sinned and fall short. It was always God who is righteous and He was the Giver of gifts. 

We ought to remember always that God’s intention, His commandments, His heart and goal was to save mankind although we cannot fully know the mind of the Lord but from reading the word of God, and the experience or teachings from the apostles, we should not stray far from that. May our Lord Jesus Christ help us to live a life with humility knowing our place in Him and lead both gentiles and our Jewish brothers and sisters so that they may know God.
