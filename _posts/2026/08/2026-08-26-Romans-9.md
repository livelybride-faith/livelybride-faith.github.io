Have you ever have this experience where you were counting the days of how you were freed from certain addictions, feeling a bit proud of the achievements, only to fall right back to zero? It’s disheartening and discouraging, some gave up and let all hell break loose, some try again and stuck in that loop of falling again. It is futile to depend on our own strength to achieve salvation. Some may be confused that one must be holy to saved, that is a deception. Jesus Christ came to saved sinners not to condemn them but it doesn’t mean that we can abuse the grace and sin intentionally, as Paul tried to explain the gospel of Christ, salvation to the readers of the book of Romans, he touched on one important point, that is God is God. 

God is the Maker and Creator, He can and has the right to choose who or what to accomplish His will that is the salvation of mankind. It doesn’t mean that God just set a destiny on a certain person that they are destined or defined forever that way, but God gave people the space and time to grow, to learn and the chances for them to repent from sin. People always learn from mistakes, rarely by theory, because theory is just in the mind and empty words, only when one starts to do something and act upon the theory then only the experience will form. That’s why when we read the bible, we ought to act upon the word of God as well to do what He commanded. So when we do that, we learn, we grow and we should thank God for the grace and mercy He shown us when we made mistakes. Now link that to the old testament, people kept asking then did they not do what God asked, they prayed, served, sacrificed, donated, but still did not attain salvation, it’s because they think that they can earn salvation thru all that, deceived themselves that they are holier or better than others. 

This then lead to another delusion of perception that they think that those who does not follow the law or rituals are not worthy of salvation. Did God destroy them all? Both those who lead people to sin by thinking that works leads to salvation or those who sin by believing that works leads to salvation? Are not the Israelites or Judaism and those who are in deception still operating in their sins? It’s been thousands of years, if God was without mercy, how does one explain then that these still exists? This is how it is shown and the evidence of God is a merciful God, He is still merciful after all those years, He has shown patience over time and allowing space for the people to grow and repent. It was clearly shown in the life of Pharaoh of how God does not force people to change their hearts, He teaches and nurture but He never force. It’s up to people to choose and have faith in Him. 

As for those who are now in Christ, how can then we boast about our salvation? If we are still asking the question of who is worthy and who is not worthy to be saved, then we are still sitting at the judgement seat that belongs to God, I say this in the context of eternal salvation not of morality or ethics. We as children of God cannot escape nor avoid our responsibility that is to judge not by appearance but by righteous judgement. God did not ask us not to judge, He said to judge not by appearance but by righteous judgement. Jesus Christ came to save sinners, every human being is a sinner, no one is good, not even one, with that in mind, we are always humbled by this fact that Jesus Christ is the Saviour. 

Paul wanted his brothers and sisters, his own flesh and blood, the Jewish community to be saved by faith in Christ to the point that he wishes that if by cutting off his own salvation that they can be saved, he would choose that. However it does not work that way, it’s only to point out how eager he was for the Jewish community to attain eternal salvation by faith thru Jesus Christ. Let us pray that may God help our brothers and sisters in the Jewish community and those who are still in deception to be set free by His truth, that they can accept and receive the salvation thru faith in our Lord Jesus Christ.
