This chapter is another adventure that show the providence, provision and the faithfulness of God. Paul and other prisoners were required to be transferred to Italy and the journey was treacherous and full of hardship, the crew had to fast while sailing thru the storm, I can only imagine it’s worse for some prisoners. They felt hopeless and were discouraged, but an angel of God came to Paul and strengthen them by telling them that God has granted safety to all those who are sailing with him. I can’t help but see the resemblance of how our Lord Jesus Christ were able to stop the storm, feed the crowd, pray for their deliverance and healing that were recorded in the Gospels. Paul was doing the similar things that Jesus Christ was doing as well. 

God is a faithful God, if He had promise something, He would do it without wavering and without partiality. What amazed me is the centurion, how he was able to choose life when under pressure by the soldiers to kill the prisoners. He told those who are able to swim to go ahead first then the rest can follow by using the planks and pieces of the ship. God fulfilled His promises, they all survived and landed safely on land. 

This chapter reminds us of the faithfulness of God and encourage us not to give up even in the most hopeless situation, as God will make a way, but this is in the context of being commissioned by God Himself. If God has asked us to do something or to serve Him in some field, then we can hold on to His promise that He will protect us and provide for us. 

May God help us to be courageous and always remember to trust in Him for He is faithful and full of mercy.
