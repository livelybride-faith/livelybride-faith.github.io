Now Paul was able to speak and defend for himself after King Agrippa gave him permission. Note that the way Paul spoke in the presence of the king appointed by the Emperor of Rome. Paul spoke in a manner of respect and beg for the king’s patience, in a way that he is asking to let him finish his speech and without disturbance. It must have been a great opportunity as mostly in the synagogues or other places, he would be disturbed or cut off by the people who opposed God, albeit some did not realized it. This time he was able to finish the whole story before being labeled insane by Festus. 

He told of his experience of how he was persecuting Christians under the authority of the chief priests. He told of his regrets of his mistakes for locking up the saints and even condemning some to death then punished them and force them to blaspheme, to the point that he chase them out of the cities. Then he told them how Jesus Christ appeared to him and from that point onwards, his eyes can finally see the truth and that he had been against God. So he repented and was appointed by our Lord Jesus Christ to also open other’s eyes so that they too, may see the truth. At this point, for those who have lived long enough, should know that he wasn’t just acting nor boasting, for truly there’s no benefit at all in the worldly view to preach about our Lord Jesus Christ except for the salvation by grace thru faith in God. That was why Festus thought that Paul became insane by much study… 

A person who is always thinking about benefits & pros for themselves will never be able to understand self sacrifice, they will not be able to comprehend nor believe that there is a God or someone to sacrifice themselves for others for they are selfish themselves. The solution is ultimately God’s grace. Paul knew how some of his Jewish brothers in the community were thinking, he knew their ways as he was trained in the strictest sect of Judaism. He’s the perfect missionary to share the gospel of Christ for both the Jews and the Gentiles as he used to comply with the religious duties and traditions in the synagogues but converted after our Lord Jesus Christ appeared to him. Agrippa almost became a Christian after listening to his testimony. They too decided that Paul did nothing deserving of death and would have been set free if he did not appeal to the emperor. 

Paul was ashamed & regretted what he did to the Christians, but he did not stop there, he repented, turned away from sinning against God and rectify the wrongs he did. Though he cannot revive the dead, nor undo the mistake and take back all the hurt and pain he caused others, he moved forward and continuously serving God in ways that he was able in every given opportunity to the point of death threats. He did not drown himself in the self-pity pool nor give up but he relied on God to give him strength daily, praying fervently to help him to finish the race. Even when he was deemed insane, he kept going. 

Let us pray that may God help us to also finish the race that is set before us, give us strength and provide for us, by His grace that we can fulfill His will that was set upon us.
