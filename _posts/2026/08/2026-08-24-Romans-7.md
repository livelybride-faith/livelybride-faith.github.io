title: Romans 7 - Futility in self-reliance; Salvation thru Christ
tag: Romans
---
This chapter explained why we cannot be saved by law, Paul talks about the struggles of trying to obey the law thru our own will, determination, but somehow we will sin and so he too understood the torture of not wanting to sin but did it anyway. It just shows how powerless a human is, and how it’s impossible for human to save themselves thru law. All this explanation and the analogy of the marriage, binding and freeing before and after the death of the husband to a woman, it’s to bring out the glory of God, that only our Lord Jesus Christ can save us. It is only thru Jesus Christ that one can be saved. 

Some may argue then it’s the fault of law that causes one to sin, no the law only exist to protect the weak for the sake of peace in a society. God wants His children to live in peace, without the law everyone doing their own thing according to their own desire without order only leads to chaos. A chaotic environment does not promote growth, first there should be a standard of what can be done, what cannot be done in order that people can feel safe after knowing the boundary. Then does the boundary exist so that people can break it? Most people will find it hard to fight the urge to break the boundaries, and often times, they failed in the end and once they break a boundary, they will just keep on breaking because they succeed once. It’s like addiction to any unhealthy substance too, one may think that they are powerful enough to resist, but as Paul explained, if one become slave to sin, then the desire is to fulfill the works of sin, when one become slave to God, then the desire is to fulfil the works of God. We are either slave to sin or slave to God, one leads to death, another to life. The power to choose is still in our hands. 

Paul did not merely rebuke his fellow Jews and Greeks brothers and sisters in Christ but to share his own experience in struggling against sin as well. He found that when he wanted to do what is good, evil lies close at hand, it is hard to do good, inconvenient at most times. It may seem like it cost more now to do good but in the long run it always reward back. So the whole chapter of Paul’s explanation and argument is just to bring out to all the readers that it is futile to depend on law meaning the works of law by self for salvation, that we should thank God that through our Lord Jesus Christ that we can be saved from this impossible struggle in flesh and spirit. May God help us to set our mind on the things of the Spirit.
