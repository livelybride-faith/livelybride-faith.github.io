This letter to the saints, all beloved of God’s in Rome by Paul, to which he addressed himself as a servant of Christ Jesus, apostle, set apart for the gospel of God. This is intentional as he put himself in a position as a servant first before expanding his position as an apostle, and even then he did not boast on that but to bring out the meaning of the apostleship that is to be set apart for the purpose to share the gospel of God. With the greetings, he put so much effort and emphasize on our Lord Jesus Christ, from David to the resurrection, to bring the readers to attention of how important it is regarding Jesus Christ and not himself nor others. 

Then he prayed for them as an opening as he is about to give them some hard truth and lecture about what has been going on amongst the believers in Rome. The theme though does not run far from the Gospel in verse 16-17. 

> 16 For I am not ashamed of the gospel; it is God’s saving power for everyone who believes,c for the Jew first and also for the Greek.* 17 For in it the righteousness of God is revealed through faith for faith, as it is written, “The one who is righteous will live by faith.”,

Idolatry and adultery seems to go hand in hand, intertwined in the traits and habits once one fall into idolatry. Though Paul was trying to remind them the consequences of the idolatry that happened in the old testament when Moses was in the high mountain, Aaron the priest led the people to worship a golden calf made by human hands, it’s not only in this image we are to interpret but also to reflect deeply in our beliefs as follower of Christ. 

There has been much idolatry in the culture of Christianity as well as Judaism where people are worshipping or kneeling down before the image of creation made by human hands, such as statues, pictures or charms & amulets. They give honor to these rather than giving honor to God who is the Creator and Paul said in their claiming to be wise, they became fools. Many tried to twist and turn with much of their excuses to downplay what is actually happening in cathedrals and synagogues filled with so much images and statues of creation, how is that different from pagan idols? Due to this, the salt has lost its flavor and so begins the spiritual backsliding without any influence at all in a church. This is outright hypocrisy, preaching against idolatry but their actions shows the opposite, how can one expect others to follow them and then cry about being rejected or mocked at for being fruitless in their evangelism or mission. 

What happened next then comes the confusion of natural use of flesh intercourse that God has created for good and for multiplication of human beings. God made man and woman for marriage, not man for man nor woman for woman. This is the truth, though we love others as ourselves but when it comes to truth, we pray for them and ask God to help reveal it to them. We do not give up loving them but to show them the right way to life. 

Back to those who know God’s decrees and yet still lead others to sin as they lost their sharpness or discernment in between the line of worshipping & honoring God with creation, they lead others to hell and so they themselves being led to hell as well. Their mind become unfit and filled with every kind of injustice, evil, covetousness, malice, envy, murder, strife, deceit, craftiness, gossips, slanders, God-haters, insolent, haughty, boastful, inventors of evil, rebellious towards parents, foolish, faithless, heartless, ruthless, and they do it with the excuse or by misusing the name of God. those how know God’s decree and practice it deserve to die. That’s how serious it is for one to sin after knowing it. This is in the context of after receiving our Lord Jesus Christ that one had already accepted our Lord Jesus Christ as their saviour, so this is not about salvation. It is about being unrepentant. 

That’s how harsh the letter of Paul is but if one read it with a gentle heart and heed his warnings and heart-felt advise, one would turn away from all these and start to live a life that is worthy of Christ. May God bless His words and help us all including the readers of this chapter to be filled with His love and Holy Spirit, strengthen us so that we may live a life pleasing to Him.
