Paul continued to lead the readers of his letter into the center of what he wanted to convey, that is Christ Jesus. He said that there’s no longer condemnation for those in Christ Jesus not because of infinite grace that allows anyone to sin and be self-serving, but to address the issue of his Jewish brothers and sisters that were still very much bound to the law and have yet to breakthrough in their mindset for they were still stuck in the matters of the flesh, the rituals and religious regulations of law regarding circumcision, cleansing, food, the keeping of certain holy days. He wanted to set them free as well for he was once them. 

It is only natural when we set our minds on one thing, all that we can think of, or act on was towards that direction. For example, if our goal is to protect someone from harm, like a baby to be born soon, we will try our best to baby-proof our house and surroundings. Everything that goes thru our mind is the worst case scenarios that we play in our mind and so we try our best to do something to prevent that from happening. Another example is roller-blading, or learning to cycle, often times the trick is to focus our eyes on one point far away, where we want to arrive and when we want to break. We focus on that point and our body will naturally follow and do the actions that required to balance ourselves. We do not look down on the ground but we look forward. That is how Paul was trying to teach to set our mind on the things of the Spirit. 

Sometimes people may get lost in the process that’s why we need to read the bible everyday to remind ourselves of what exactly is the things of the Spirit. It’s a lifetime of practice, a lifestyle and habit to incorporate into our lives just like prayers as well. It’s inevitable that we ought to set goals for our lives, daily, weekly, monthly, yearly in the context of so that we are moving towards something, the purpose is not only for the sake of setting goals and achieving goals, but also to not get distracted. It’s okay though sometimes we do stray away, it’s not the end, we just have to realign ourselves again, but how will the alignment happen if we do not allow room for that? Hence, again, read the bible every day. Even if it’s just one chapter or a few verses, God will use that. 

Paul said set our mind on the things of the Spirit, he did not say since Christ died for our sins then we can just continue to live a sinful life. This is in the context of after receiving the gift of salvation by grace from God thru Christ Jesus our Lord. The gift of salvation cannot be earned, everyone is the same, it is given as a gift and no one can be saved except thru Jesus Christ our Lord. No one can be saved thru good deeds but does it mean that we stop doing good? No, instead we change our ways from wickedness to righteousness. So now there’s another problem for when we try to do righteousness, often times there’s no instant reward. This is the part where Paul encouraged us to put our hope in the things that cannot be seen now. Even when we are rejected or condemned in doing good or act righteously from our heart for the sake of Christ, we may be misunderstood, reviled, mocked, outcasted but there’s hope because nothing will be able to separate us from the love of God in Christ Jesus our Lord.
