Paul continue to rebuke from the perspective of God, which at the end of this chapter, he mentioned it is the heart that matters. He condemned hypocrisy amongst the Jewish community for they have put the yoke of the law & rituals on others such as circumcision to be saved, he knew how his Jewish brothers think and so he was trying their thought patterns to untwist their crookedness of interpretation of the will of God. It was recorded that after deconsecration of idols, the silver and gold was stripped from the idols to reuse for their own sake, they could also sell metals or resources to the pagans to build the idols or temple. That was the explanation given for the verse “you who abhor idols, do you rob temples?”  in reference to Dt. 7:25f where the teachers of the law who teach others about the law sin against the law. This was how Paul brought out the deep layers of hypocrisy in them. 

The main context of the chapter is that Paul wanted to bring out the importance of true righteousness, which is inwardly, from the heart. Deceptions always comes in layers and layers of lies to cover other facets of lies and in order to keep the lie from being exposed, another lie is created to cover it. Hence the need of help from our Lord Jesus Christ and the mercy from God to help anyone that seek Him to be untangled from the messy tangled ball of threads. See, when a person greed over something for example the gold or silver on an idol, they try to use multiple excuse or reason to justify their actions, by fooling themselves too into the deception of using it for the good of others. This is in the context of an example where a mafia knows what they are doing are against the law, unrighteous, crooked, harmful to people, say, drug trafficking. Then in the process of this, they kill, steal, rape & remove threats including law enforcers & the innocents or even use the kids based on their need for survival. Then they go to a priest and give them a large sum of money and confess their sins to the priest. The priest forgave them in the position as a god, and then they repeat this 100 times to no repentance at all. Remember when one got caught, they are required to confess their sins, just like the adulterer woman, Jesus Christ Himself did not condemn, but forgave, and then there’s one action that’s most important. He said “sin no more”, in other example, Jesus Christ also warned do not sin anymore lest a greater judgement comes. Base on this story, it is still happening in some of the churches, catholic or protestant or other denominations. The point is that this hypocrisy happens everywhere. 

Paul wrote this letter to pinpoint on just one fixed community that is the Jewish community in Rome back then, but if we read it in a way that it’s only for the Roman Jewish back then, then we would miss the principle that he was trying to convey. The message of this chapter is for hypocrites and the same for everyone who seek God and righteousness, it is true righteousness from the heart that matters. May God help us all to not miss the mark & to always walk on the righteous path according to His will from a pure heart and to create in us a clean heart.
