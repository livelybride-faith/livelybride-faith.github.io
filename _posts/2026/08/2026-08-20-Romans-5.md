Have you ever experience while doing something good out of a pure heart just wanting to help but were misunderstood and when something went wrong, people accused you or blame you for the mistakes? Or just because you did not do something according to the  method that they think you should do and gave you hell? I believe many has gone thru that, and our Lord Jesus Christ did too. Just because He came to the earth to save mankind thru dying on the cross, the most humiliated way to die as the crucifixion was meant for criminals, so the people were thinking how can a hero be a criminal? How can a Son of God, a God be a criminal? How can a powerful mighty God that creates everything die without any defense system at all? That was why the chief priest wanted to hide the resurrection of our Lord Jesus Christ. They tried so hard to cover it up by buying false witnesses. The more they tried to cover it, the more that it is true. What is the truth? God proves His love for us in that, as we were still sinners, Christ died for us. 

The sin of Adam, which is thru one man, is the cause of death of all mankind even for those who did not sin because of the curse. Can the sin be wiped away or be forgiven without any effort from mankind? No, because of the law, when law exists, it reveals the sin. God’s one commandment for Adam is to not eat the fruit from the tree of good and evil. That was the only law and that was how the sin of Adam was revealed. He can eat everything else, there’s so many other types of fruits that he can eat but the serpent knew that one law which will cause the fall of Adam. So he tempt and bait in order to cause a break and division in between God and man. God wanted to redeem mankind and so thru grace, He sent our Lord Jesus Christ, who was without sin, came to earth and offered Himself up as a sacrifice to atone for the sins of mankind. This is not cruelty because He proved His love for us in that while we were still sinners. 

Have you ever experience that sometimes we know that it’s our fault that some mistake happened and then suddenly one day, it was revealed to you, but you were never punished nor exposed, then you feel that conviction in your heart, that God knew all along? Yet, I was given the chance over and over again? To those who experience it, one shall know how humbling that felt. Yet, the awe towards God is much more than the shame because of His love. No, this does not apply to continuous abusive relationship nor taking grace for granted after multiple times of rebuke. For those who really do not know right from wrong were blinded and those who knew will always find a way to cover it and live in fear of being found out. That was what happened to Adam after he ate the forbidden fruit, he knew that he sinned. 
The history of the whole old testament speaks of God’s unfailing love in which over and over again, He tried to lead human back to Him even after numerous times where people proved to Him that they would betray Him and they did. So trespass increased because law came in, but because of that much sin, grace abounded all the more. Grace is when we deserve punishment for our actions, we did not. We did not deserve gifts, but we receive it anyway. For all my time when committing idolatry, I have never heard of any false gods that would die for my sin, not one. Not one of them can forgive my sins too. Most of it just told me to do good or I will get punished. Some of it told me not to feel, not to see, not to listen, not to speak. Just be dead. Thank God that our Lord Jesus Christ save us all from such false gods, He came to give us life, to desire righteousness, to stand up for the weak, help and be life giving to those who need it. 

Back to the message of Romans 5, we are therefore justified by faith, we have peace with God thru our Lord Jesus Christ, reconciled. Even though thru one man, Adam, the never-ending death came, but thru one Man, Jesus Christ, eternal life too, arrived.
