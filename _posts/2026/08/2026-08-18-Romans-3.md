title: Romans 2 - True & righteous judgement from God

---
In this chapter, Paul’s argument on upholding righteousness from the heart took a turn, it was to address the boasting of the Jewish community, so this create another issue where people starts to twist that law does not matter anymore for grace from God is enough. This is in the context of after accepting our Lord Jesus Christ, not the salvation and even if it’s not in the context of salvation, our conscience works in our hearts to know that it is wrong to kill. There may be some exceptions where some people lack that due to their upbringing or conditioning. Are they lost then? Only God knows, for God is the righteous judge, He will judge according to His will. Back to the context of this letter, Paul wanted to address both sides, actually all sides of perspective but right now it’s narrowed down to Jewish and Gentiles, Jewish on their insist in salvation thru law by circumcision, Gentiles in their insist in salvation thru faith without any law. In modern words, unlimited grace, unconditional forgiveness, sinning without consequences, prosperity gospels, offsetting sins by donation and etc. All these words comes from one root that is twisting the meaning of the grace of God. 

Grace is given, trust is earned, forgiveness only happens in true repentance. When we ask forgiveness from God, we already know at that moment whether we mean it or not. Some repeat in weakness, some intentionally, some due to injustice in their lives, some due to their circumstances, some addiction. So are these doomed to death? According to Paul’s letter in chapter 1, by law, yes. In this kind of situation, who else can live then? See what he did there, he just bring all the audience to a point to realized that truly no one can be saved if it depends on works. It requires grace from God, that no one can boast at all. No on in this earth can boast about how righteous or how holy, or how zealous or passionate towards God because no one is good. If it wasn’t for God, who can receive salvation and starts to live a holy life? Both group, the Jewish and the Gentiles, each in their own twisted perception on law and faith, it is due to the striving of each other, fearing that one is not better than the other. So in order to hide the insecurity or the humiliation that each other has imposed on, both sides boast either on law or on faith so that they feel superior over each other. Paul was actually trying to disarm striving in between both sides. We can read that more in the book of Galatians, which is also written by Paul, stating clearly, there is not longer Jew or Greek. In that context, the Greek represent the gentiles. 

When people starts to claim what is from God, the mind became twisted, perception blurred, judgment clouded and this leads to people trying to justify their actions thru excuses and unrighteous judgement base on their feelings. However our Lord Jesus Christ taught us to judge by righteous judgement. So without true repentance, people will start to deceive themselves even deeper and gets further and further away from truth. An example would be, believers who kept sinning in one area that goes to masses, church and serving actively thinking that they are better and holier than others or just by having that position or title. It’s sad because they do not realize that, and when that root was not address properly to stop sinning, the more guilty they feel, some respond by withdrawal, some by serving more, some by praying more, some by humiliating others or lead others to the same sin so they can feel better because others also sin. So what then do we stop doing all the works? 

When we try to justify & judge with our own understanding, then we are already missing the mark, so Paul was trying to lead both back to a central point, that is God. God will justify both the circumcised and the uncircumcised base on that same faith. It all boils down into one point, the heart, the intention, why do we do what we do? Why do you go to church? Why do you serve? Why do you pray? Why do you donate? What is it that you’re trying to get out of what you do? These are the questions that are worth exploring in our journey of faith. Do not be discouraged when our intentions are still wrong, the point that Paul wrote the letters to the community in Rome was to lead them back to truth, if one is truly repentance, God is waiting with open arms to let us rely on His grace. May our Lord Jesus Christ help us all.
