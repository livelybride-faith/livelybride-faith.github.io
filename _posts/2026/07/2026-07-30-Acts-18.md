Paul kept on proclaiming the word and this time in Corinth after leaving Athens. He was supporting himself by working as a tentmaker but every Sabbath he would go into the synagogue and argue with the Jews and Greeks in order that they maybe convinced that our Lord Yeshua is the Messiah. When they refused, he just shook the dust from his clothes and moved on to the gentiles. It seems that this might have discouraged him or led him to fear as the Lord spoke to him in a vision to strengthen him to continue to speak without fear. 

Paul as zealous as one can be, still was a human and susceptible to fear and doubt. When we serve Christ in ministries or family or church or workplace or wherever it is and sometimes it doesn’t work out or obstructed, it’s tempting to give up and stop because of fear and doubt. Did God really want me to do this? Did God really bless this ministry? Did God really put that burden in my heart to be here? Just like Paul did, he was a person with a brain and heart that can feel emotions. Paul did not give up but was all the more bolder and indeed God did not let him be harmed. However one must pay attention to this context, it’s about proclaiming the good news of our Lord Jesus Christ. It’s never about us, it’s about Christ and the salvation of the person who’s in the proximity of our influence if it might help them to get closer to God. We must never give up proclaiming the gospel, that is the salvation thru faith in Jesus Christ by God’s grace and mercy. 

After that, Paul went back to Antioch and his goal was still to proclaim that our Lord Yeshua is the Messiah to the Jews first then strengthen all the disciples. Paul’s time with Priscilla and Aquila provided another platform or opportunity for Apollos to be able to preach and to understand the way of God more accurately. This has become another missionary team that spread the gospel with accuracy because he was showing that Yeshua is the Messiah thru scriptures, which is the old testament. 

The chapter serves as an encouragement that the work for Christ is continuous, dangerous sometimes but with protection from God; and if God said do not be afraid, then let us go on and proclaim the gospel of our Lord Yeshua is the Messiah. Sometimes it might feel like a mundane, repetitive, or seems to be fruitless, but don’t give up, trust in God and keep on planting the seed. Take comfort in how others have responded to the message as well even when those who we want them to believe has yet to respond.
