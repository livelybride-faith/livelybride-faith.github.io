There was a false teaching from Judea which is against God and the message that our Lord Jesus Christ has been preaching, that is to be saved, people have to be circumcised! No! Only thru accepting our Lord Jesus Christ can one be saved! Those who taught the false teachings were the believers from the sect of the Pharisees. Peter voiced up and corrected them when they called a meeting among the apostles and elders, told them about how God had chosen him where the gentiles would hear the message of the good news and become believers. This is to disarm the division in between who is worthy or not according to bloodlines or sects. 

The salvation is for everyone and those who are willing to accept our Lord Jesus Christ and believe in Him. After Peter explained it, then they were willing to keep silent and listened to Barnabas and Paul as they told of all the signs and wonders that God had done through them among the the gentiles enforcing the the testimony that God indeed has endorsed and His will for the gentiles to be saved as well. 
Maybe the Pharisees were afraid of change, or of losing control over people that they had relied on to feel powerful over others, or maybe it was hurting the business of the matters involved in circumcision. Religious laws has been infamous to oppress people but Jesus Christ is famous to set people free with truth. They too were stuck in the limbo of the laws and grace, so Peter’s explanation with the testimony from Barnabas and Paul could have help them to breakthrough the mindset. 

Then a letter was written with the consent of the whole church to send Barnabas and Paul to impose only to abstain from what has been sacrificed to idols and from blood and from what is strangled and from sexual immorality. They were commission and now they receive a mission to go and correct the wrong teachings. Of course, as always when there’s a mission, there’s spiritual warfare and caused division among the team as Barnabas and Paul parted ways due to the disagreement of letting John Mark to follow. 

Again, Barnabas displayed the quality or trait of believing in the underdogs or willing to nurture a nobody into a somebody that can glorify God. He succeed as John Mark wrote one of the four gospels and in the later letters of Paul, he approved of John Mark and said he was useful for the kingdom. Even when there’s a split or seems that a negative thing happened but the history proven otherwise, the gospel and truth spread even wider, instead of one mission team, there were two teams. The end became a new beginning. 

God’s providence is in the picture as people try to serve Him in weakness of men but with God, the gospel reached the gentiles and now look at the whole world, Christians are everywhere regardless of hidden or not. It’s encouraging as what seems to be the end is a a start of a beginning of a new chapter.
