title: Acts 20 - Repentance towards God and faith towards our Lord Jesus Christ

Paul was eager to go to Jerusalem so that he may reach by day of Pentecost, it’s an important holy day for the Jewish people, even more so for Christians after the ascension of our Lord Jesus Christ for that is the day when the power of the Holy Spirit pour down on the apostles and believers. He knew that his ministry in Ephesus has come to an end as he had to continue another mission in Jerusalem so he gathered the elders to speak to them one last time in order that they may remember the teachings that he passed to them from our Lord Jesus Christ. 

First, he told them clearly that he’s not responsible for any of their blood as he had done all he could to proclaim the good news to them. It’s in the context of spreading the good news to them, it was extremely important to him as he was the one that persecuted the church of Christ in the past and so he felt all the more the burden of his hands being defiled by the blood of his sins. Then he exhorted them to watch over the flocks, to be a good shepherd in the context of preventing the flocks from false teachings. He urged them to be an example like him, to work and support himself & the others who are weaker. This is commendable in the sense that there are people who are sick or disabled or at an old age that wasn’t their fault and the family members who have healthy body and able to work. The healthy and the strong shall work and provide for those who cannot provide for themselves. This is a great blessing, for our Lord Jesus Christ has said “It is more blessed to give than to receive”. 

In the near end of one’s life, knowing that this may be the last time to see each other, what will you say to each other? Surely the most important things for there’s only so much time. It is vital to always remember the good news, the truth, that is the gift of salvation by repentance towards God and faith towards our Lord Jesus Christ.
