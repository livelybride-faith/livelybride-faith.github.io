Now Paul went to a place that was quite crucial for the development of Christian church as he found Timothy there. A young man, son of a Jewish woman but of a Greek man. It’s a combination that without true love for each other that cannot last nor prevail in marriage. Timothy learned from both world, the faith in God from his mom, the culture that was crucial for relatability in Greek community from his dad. Then now Paul, the Pharisee of the Pharisee, converted, transformed, zealous for God that none can stop him for the sake of Christ to nurture him into someone that can lead a church in the future after his passing. Timothy was known for his young age so Paul encouraged him in one of his letters and the depth of their relationship was revealed and shown by his outpouring love in letters.  

Now Paul receive his mission by vision just like Peter and he obeyed it as God called him to proclaim the good news to the people in Macedonia. As usual, in a mission, there are obstacles, spiritual warfare and persecution. This time it’s thru someone who had a spirit of divination and the only thing she did was to proclaim the truth until Paul was annoyed. So Paul ordered the spirit to leave her in the name of Jesus Christ and her owner was enraged because he lost the source of income. That led them to jail but they were able to worship God and sang hymns to God! They were actually evangelizing in jail where the prisoners were inside that might not have any chance to hear the word of God outside. Imagine that, the love of God, unbound, till the extend of Him sending people into the jail filled with broken people, undeserving of love nor forgiveness, for some deserve the consequences of their sins or anti-social activities such as theft, murder or violence against the society. 

The main character was the jailer, who was afraid as the earthquake has set the stock, a locking device of both hands or head sometime came free & he thought the prisoners were gone. He thought that was the end of his life and maybe his family too, all that he built in life was gone just like that. So he decided to take his own life until Paul shouted at him in a loud voice. Paul responded with mercy and compassion for the jailer as he asked Paul and Silas what must he do to be saved? They answered, “Believe in the Lord Jesus, and you will be saved, you and your household.” That’s it, the grace & gift of salvation from the Lord Jesus Christ is thru faith & belief in Him. What an amazing grace! 

God is a God of mercy and justice, but in between the two extreme, only He can judge righteously, but I notice a pattern, it always leads to life. His mercy leads people to life, His justice also leads people to life, His commandments consolidated into two, worship God and love others as ourselves, it all leads to life because if one really truly love others, they would not do anything that might harm them. This chapter is about how the Gospel of Christ being unbound even in prison and ultimately leads to life. The truth sets people free.
