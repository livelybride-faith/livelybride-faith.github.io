Paul was someone who had special privileges as he was trained in synagogue and became someone who was very zealous of the law of Moses for God’s sake. He thought he was until he was not. He persecuted Christians and had an encounter with our Lord Jesus Christ. It wasn’t just a short encounter as he learned directly from our Lord Jesus Christ when he was at his lowest point of his stature, he couldn’t go back to the Jewish community and he wasn’t accepted by the Christians as well because of how he persecuted the Christians. He spent a long time to rebuild his credibility with the Christian Jewish community after Ananias & Barnabas accepted him. 

The response from Paul mattered a lot as he genuinely repented and did not continue to mock God’s servants. He followed Ananias’ instructions and was able to get healed by God. Paul was not only humbled but he realized that he was wrong and he repented. This is the response that was the key turning point that became a blessing to many people including his own. Genuine repentance. In contrast, remember a Simon a magician that tried to buy the Holy Spirit from Peter because he saw how it could benefit his business? Two entirely different response from two entirely different heart. Paul genuinely believed he was doing something good in the name of God, Simon the magician wanted benefits for his own selfish reason. Peter’s rebuke caused Simon the magician to be afraid of God’s power and ask Peter to pray so that none of what Peter said would happened to him, he was still afraid of losing what he had. 

This is important as he  tried to persuade his fellow Jewish brothers so that their eyes will be opened and accept our Lord Jesus Christ and to see that He truly is the Messiah that they have been waiting for. His heart was burdened and burning with that desire to have their eyes opened that they may finally see! because he had been thru that experience and he knew that some of them genuinely worship God. However, there were some who did not believed and wanted to kill him out of envy too just like how our Lord Jesus Christ been thru. So at this point, he used his citizenship born as a Roman to fulfill his mission of spreading the gospel of our Lord Jesus Christ to the gentiles. 

There was a time in my life that I did not appreciate my citizenship as Malaysian as I’m a Chinese descendant and it is not easy to be a different race in a land that is not of the Chinese majority within a muslim country. However after accepting our Lord Jesus Christ, my perspective started to change gradually, I started to see the opportunities to be able to become a living example of how a follower of Christ ought to live. It turned the bitterness into a heart filled with compassion towards our muslims friends and siblings who are all still in oppression. The moment they are born, their identification is permanently a muslim by law because they are the descendants of a muslim. They had no choice, do you know how sad is that? How painful is it for them to live without freedom to know Christ? I repent for all the things that I’ve said and thought about them before I understood. They need love all the more than we can ever imagine not hatred, not discrimination. We all do… in fact… 

So reading Paul’s experience and his citizenship, what kind of perspective did it transformed in you today about your own citizenship where God has put you in, now that you’ve also gain another citizenship of the Kingdom of God?
