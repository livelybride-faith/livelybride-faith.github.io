Paul continued his mission trip in Ephesus and taught about the Holy Spirit. A group of disciples believed in the baptism of John but have yet to hear about Lord Jesus Christ. So Paul revealed to them that the baptism of John was repentance so that people believe in Lord Jesus Christ. The baptism of our Lord Jesus Christ will allow the believers to receive the Holy Spirit. God did miracles thru Paul so that people can be healed and delivered from demons when they come into contact with the fabrics that had touched Paul before. 

Some Jewish exorcist tried to use the name of the Lord Jesus but failed and was attacked instead by the evil spirit. What happened here is that they were just using Jesus’ nam, but did not have anything as in relationship or connection nor do they believe in Jesus Christ. So, even the evil spirit know when one is just using the name of Jesus for power, glory and fame. It’s not all bad, as this led to many in Ephesus to believe in Lord Jesus Christ. 

Due to the mass conversion of Christian believers, it was hurting the business and financial status of the society in Ephesus, many were just using the name of the false gods to scam people. Those who were scammed were living in fear and terror being manipulated by all those superstitions. It was a den of wolves with sheep having no shepherd. So Paul’s evangelism caused an uproar there and almost everyone related to him or know him try to get him away from Ephesus as it was getting out of control. The scripture recorded some don’t even know why they gather there. 

This is the crowd mentality that people follow for the sake of following not knowing why or what they were following. Some was caused by the desire to belong, connection, or fear of missing out. It’s a stark reminder that in this age of mass media available 24/7 without any gates at all, that we must be vigilant of what or who we follow, does it increase hope, faith, love and lead to life? or does it just cause more anxiety? Consider a media fast and try to read the bible if there’s too much stress or overwhelmed with toxicity in life. Taking a break promotes health spiritually, mentally and emotionally as well. 

Back to the center of the message, all miracles, prophecies and works of healing and casting out demons were all for one purpose, it’s to proclaim the gospel of our Lord Jesus Christ, and to set people free so that they can worship God and not false gods. The most important thing is to build a genuine relationship with our Lord Jesus Christ, when we believe in something, we try to find out everything about them, to enjoy the journey of getting to know them and it leads to a season of sweetness in exploration & discovery. That’s how the love grew more and to lead one into worship with awe.
