title: Acts 21 - Fulfill the will of God
tag: Acts
---
Paul wanted to go to Jerusalem despite knowing the danger and how he will face his death for the sake of Christ. He knew that before others and still he wanted to go because it’s his calling. In this chapter, it is recorded that his situation and even his closest ally made it harder for him to go to Jerusalem. Not that his ally blocked him violently but with emotional pressure enough to break his heart by their weeping. These people wanted Paul to stay for their sake to keep him for themselves as they saw how beneficial it is for them to have a shepherd like Paul around them. Maybe they were afraid, maybe they wanted more time with Paul and cherished him. Alas, in the kingdom of God, it’s about sharing every good thing, while we love someone, sometimes we have to let them go if God wants to use them. Loving someone is not by holding them by our side forever but it’s to see that they have a genuine relationship with God that no matter where they go or what situation they are in, they grab hold on God and not on someone. 

A missionary leader is most successful when their leaders and members became missionaries and spreading the gospel of Christ’ gift of salvation. It’s not to collect every sheep and goats to fill up their own church. This doesn’t mean that they leave the fellowship entirely but they moved on to the place where God called them for they have learned all they could and to be commissioned / sent out to save more souls thru spreading the gospel of Christ. There are some of course that stay in the same place forever and to feed and tend the sheep just like Peter in the land of Judah. Paul was a missionary, he went all over the land of gentiles for Christ then he came back to where he planted churches, he still kept the relationship & fellowship with them. 

This chapter is a reminder that whatever we do or want in our lives, it’s vital to prioritize the will of God over ours or others. For our lives and everything else that we have were given by our God, our Creator, may our Lord Jesus Christ help us all to be able to let go of all that He wants us to let go and to fulfill His will in us. It is dangerous to treat someone or something more important than God as it may cross that line of idolatry. It’s common that believers struggle with this, even our Lord Jesus Christ struggle while He was praying at Gethsemane, in the end, He prevails and completed our Father’s will. May God help us to be able to overcome the struggles in our lives and follow our Lord Jesus Christ.
