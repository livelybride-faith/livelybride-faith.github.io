Paul continue to face persecutions and death threats no matter where he is, yet he still proclaim the gospel of Christ. Due to his origin, the Pharisee, a son of Pharisees, some scribes of the Pharisees were able to pause for a while and listen to him. They were almost convinced that by his tenacity and persistency in this matter, something must have happened just like how Joseph when he was serving in temple, an angel sent by God appeared to him and spoke to him. Surely they still have this in memory but was discounted because of constant brainwash from the elders and leaders of the synagogue. However some may be able to connect the dots one day when their eyes are opened and believe in what was spoken by God. 

All that said, of course Paul was just a man, he too had emotions and feelings, after all those ordeals of violence, he must have went to the Lord and prayed hard. For the scripture mention : “Keep up your courage!”. This time the threats became real physically as there were 40 men plotting to kill him and the whole drama of trying to get Paul to safety with 200 soldiers, 70 horsemen and 200 spearmen. This fulfilled the promises from God towards Paul. His providence led to the protection by a group of able men that can protect Paul from this assassination. 

This chapter speaks of God’s promises and protection as the tough gets going. However able Paul may be, or persistent in his mission, without God’s intervention and help from other people, he might have been killed. God did not allow that to happen due to His love for the gentiles as He was about to transform the whole Roman empire. As history recorded, Christianity spread to the whole world but we must not repeat the same mistakes of violence and bloodshed. Just look at this part here, God did not kill those who opposed Him but protected those who is with Him. He did not condone violence with violence but still able to achieve to spread the gospel the non-violent ways. 

May our Lord Jesus Christ help us to be able to live out a persistent life filled with the Holy Spirit and continue to spread the gospel of Christ whenever the chances arises.
