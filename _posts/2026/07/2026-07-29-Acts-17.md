Now Paul and Silas went about Thessalonica to proclaim and to correct his Jewish brothers and sisters in the synagogue for three Sabbath days in other word, three weeks. He argued from a position of love to win them over because he was a Pharisee of the Pharisee, which means that he knew the scriptures in ways that people misinterpret or mis-apply, and he knew how to speak their language in terms of lingo, so he could leverage on revealing the truth from the scripture to them. Many were persuaded because they finally saw the truth! Some Jews became jealous because of that, they lost followers, control, power, source of income maybe, benefits, influence and they might think that it Paul and Silas came to steal it. In fact, they were the ones that stole from God to lead the sheep astray, God just sent His missionaries to lead the sheep back to Him. 

In contrast, at Beroea, the Jews were more receptive and were willing to study the scriptures daily with eagerness and they examined it just like many pastors & teachers in church doing exegetical and expository studies in scriptures. Many too believed but some did not. 

Then in Athens, the capital of Greece and the city-state of Attica, a city full of idols, and idols temples where the people were very spiritual. This group of people have all sorts of philosophy and they spent most of their time in nothing but telling or hearing something new, sounds like news station, or social media in modern days just spreading false news and information. Paul, just like some of us now, had to deal with all the falsehood and now we have AI slop too, but okay, we do not despair, we filter and sieve, then try our best to proclaim the truth that the so called gods like Poseidon, Zeus, Athena and so on, they are not God. Our God, the one true God who made the world and everything in it, He who is Lord of heaven and earth, does not live in shrines made by human hands! For in Him we live and move and have our being! So the deity is nothing like gold or silver or stone formed by art or imagination of mortals… 

God did not destroy them all, He wants them to know the truth and repent! Just like how I was, in the past, an idolator, converted to accept our Lord Jesus Christ as my Saviour and Lord, repent and stop worshipping those idols, got rid of the statues and amulets, for now my God, our God, lives in me now, but of course apart from Him, I’m nothing! All those wasted resources spent on idolatry and fengshui or metaphysics or magic or witchcraft or divination or even human idols or saints living in statue?! They stole from God and created futility in people’s lives. May God help us all, mankind of the earth to see the truth and be set free! Do you know how free it was to no longer need to wear the heavy necklace of amulets because of fear? Do you know how free it is to no longer bow down to the false idols in temples because of terror? I say this not because of disrespect of people’s faith or religion but it’s the motives of the faith! It’s about the fear and terror being controlled and deceived when I was looking for the TRUE GOD. JESUS CHRIST set me free! 

Sometimes those material things may help ease or comfort the feeling of being far from God, so people use that to feel near to God, as if we can control God, as if that will help us to reach God faster, easier or deeper. This might offend a lot of people but this is the truth, that often times we search and seek for a god because we don’t feel close to God, but thru the belief and acceptance of our Lord Jesus Christ, we are reconciled to God but truly though He is not far from each of us. May God set all those who are bound by fear and terror to freedom in love and peace.
