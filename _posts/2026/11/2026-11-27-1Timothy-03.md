Paul gave Timothy a specific set of instructions from the principles of God, our Lord Jesus Christ taught all these in the gospels too. Some churches adhered to these set of rules strictly in order to protect the body of Christ, some though uses it to demean and control people. So it is vital that each case is taken into context in order to judge righteously. 

For example, married only once means loyalty and faithful to covenant, it doesn’t mean that you have to withstand an abusive marriage that causes harm and generational mental health issues to your family which may lead to another generational tragedy. Gentle doesn’t mean that you can sacrifice truth over love. Keeping children submissive does not mean that a parent can oppress them or threaten them into being submissive, often times relationship that produce the fruit of being submissive in love comes from a lot of quality time and effort and from those who are not hypocrites. Respect is earned and not given, it’s not very complicated but it is not easy to achieve. We all know it ourselves in our hearts, when someone wronged you in a way that others cannot see but you can and you know, then the respect and trust is lost. You know that the person is a hypocrite and you know that they are no longer trustworthy. 

Now turn that around and picture that for yourself, if you do that to others, then people will know too. This is in the context of a setting in being a minister because of the massive crowd that they meet with, and the responsibility that they hold. With much influence and power, comes great responsibility. However, it doesn’t mean that a minister is not a person and that they are perfect, but that’s the standard set by Paul for Timothy to setup a team of ministers in a church. For Timothy is young and lack of social experience so I’d imagine Paul would have to set all these standards and rules in order that he has something to compare with when choosing his team. There are a lot of hurtful traumatized Christians and ex-Christians that left the church because of this exact issues that were caused by hypocrisy and spiritual abuse. They have cause their brothers and sisters to fall and God will hold each of them accountable. 

For many of those who left the church or community, they did not lose faith in God, they only lose faith in people. When it is impossible to change others, what do people do? Some choose violence, some choose to leave. So if your church is getting smaller and smaller in number, ask your conscience and ask God, what’s going on with your leaders. Do not be fooled into thinking that the grace of God is enough, if you are a leader of a church, you should know that you are hold accountable. With that in mind, I do not think that any person who truly revere God will take up this position or calling lightly. Many of the ministers left the field due to the imbalance and abuse they received from their leaders and people of the church as well. 

As people often take everything for granted and become entitled. So now, this comes into picture, leaders have their role to play, as well as believers. It’s not a one way street in a body of Christ pointing fingers at each other but this is reminder that before and after or while one is pointing fingers at others, remember to point it backwards to ourselves, that was why Paul said to pray for leaders, pray for each other, give thanks and pray fervently at all times. When we’re busy praying, we’ll turn our focus towards Jesus Christ, maybe it will begin with bitterness and anger but just like King David and the Psalmist, they often end with praise and awe towards God. 

The church of the Living God is not perfect, that’s why we need God, may our Lord Jesus Christ helps us all, let God’s will be done on earth as it is in heaven.
