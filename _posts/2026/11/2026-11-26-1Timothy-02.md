This chapter is one of the hardest chapter I’ve tried to read and write in all my years in the journey of faith, it’s difficult because no matter how it’s worded, people are going to cherry pick and interpret it as they desire, not because some intentionally do it, but due to the complexity in their upbringing and a person’s experience and how they were conditioned to perceive or influenced. What I do believe is that these letters to Timothy were greatly connected to the culture and what happened in Ephesus which some of it were recorded in book of Acts. 

First, the background, Ephesus was a place where an idol, in the image of a female that influenced the circle of religion, economy, civic and political and then finally leading the community to be socially coercive because when one does not follow the crowd, they would be deem the heretics in their society, outcasted and do not belong. What happened in the book of Acts was Paul cast out a demon from a woman that was annoying him which then cause the master of that woman to lose their income. There are two ways to look at it, one is that a female is weaker in the sense that can be deceive just like when the serpent deceive Eve, another way is that a female’s unique gift or trait by God is the ability to sense spirits, falsehood and truth. What people call them sometimes are the common word “sixth sense”, or husbands would know the experience that the wife knows it whatever that they tried to hide. When this is not used wisely then it become a problem or a male’s ego is hurt because of how the exposure was shameful; but then again, exposure always comes with shame or at a certain degree of embarrassment, it is up to the person whether they are humble or not. I like to see it as a gift and I think Paul had the same direction in his mind although not stated clearly. 

Paul knew that the oppression of manhood in Ephesus might have caused extreme rage, anger, violence due to the influence of the whole society from that idolatry. He wanted Timothy to stand firm and these two letters are clear, directive toned instructions. However we must understand the letters in context where Paul and Timothy had some understanding that was not disclosed in history nor in the Holy Bible, and Paul learned from Christ in terms of principles of God, not a rigid rule book. Then Timothy was his son in faith, what some called is his spiritual son, I wanted to avoid that wording because some would think that in terms of entities, but what I wanted to say was Paul treated Timothy like his son, in terms of relationship, intimacy and love. So Timothy knew that these letters were principles and not a rigid rule book. 

The context then in this specific chapter is about a prayer service held in public assembly in the name of God. Paul experienced a female who had a spirit of divination, she followed Paul and cried out, ‘These men are slaves of the Most High God, who proclaim to you the way of salvation.’ So he ordered that spirit to come out of her in the name of Jesus Christ and he was jailed afterwards. With this in context, a prayer service in public assembly no matter which religion or faith, the spirit of divination will try to work their way to create disruptions or commotions. I’ve seen this and experienced this in Christian conference, sometimes it’s godly, sometimes it’s not. 
Sometimes I was influenced by ungodly spirit, sometimes I knew it was the Holy Spirit. So I learned to discern before I open my mouth and I rarely say anything about my visions or dreams anymore because of how people looked at me like I’m crazy. So I do agree with Paul in some sense where a solution was given to someone like this is to learn quietly, in calmness. This chapter is never about forbidding women to be teachers, but it’s to encourage one to learn and discern first, how men should conduct themselves in prayer services, and both men and women should not strive against each other because there’s just no point unless it’s to fulfill the plan of the devil. Believers should be alert enough that idolatry because it’s rooted out of greed and will always cause striving and division in society. 

As for the child bearing, Paul was pointing to events of Adam and Eve where Christ was born by a woman, Mary. It’s not about salvation of a woman that can only be saved after she give birth to a child, and please don’t twist this into saying that Paul discouraged marriage. Context is important. It’s to encourage believers to think of birth of Christ and to continue in faith, love, holiness and self-control. 
So I would like to encourage all that has experience this spiritual experience, you are not crazy but we have to learn to discern and test every spirit, read and study the Bible, let Christ be our foundation and then we let the Holy Spirit guide us. God made male and female, and gave us each a specific sets of gifts and ability in order to fulfill specific sets of work in God’s kingdom. May our Lord Jesus Christ help us all to be truly rooted in God’s words and in love for the sake of gospel of Christ.
