This chapter continue to list out all the proper practices and behaviour that a Christian should live in their lives, after the husband and wife then now the household, in a company where master or employers and their slaves or employees work. This is not to encouraged slavery, it’s the culture back then and so it was written in that context. In the year 2026, slavery is frowned upon but we still serve companies in our job, position, voluntarily or paid, no matter what is the condition we serve as we serve Christ our Lord. As for the owners or leaders or masters, he warned them too to step with their threatenings for they too have the same Lord in heaven and with Him there is no partiality. 

The whole armor of God is to depict how a Christian should be cautious and alert at all times to stand against evil. In context, he used the analogy of a roman soldier’s armory but the real point is stand in truth, let our heart be righteous, wherever one goes spread the gospel of peace, guard ourselves with faith, think about the salvation that was given by God thru Christ, read the word of God and study it, hone it like honing a sword to make it sharp so that one can stand against deception and pray in the Spirit at all times in every prayer and supplication. There was once a time where false teachers enter into the church to deceive people to put on spiritual armors or weapons to fight against the spiritual world’s creatures and I too was deceived because I did not read the bible in context. I just took everything in that the church taught, I was foolish and wasted so much of God’s resources and spread the wrong teachings too. After reading and studying the bible in context, then I found that oh so this is what it meant. All those false teachings, I figured, it’s just to manipulate others and when they reached a point of where they cannot control them with the same deception, they will create another one out of nothing and people will still believe them because they trust them blindly. It’s vital to read and study the bible in context so that one do not fall into deception. Only with the Spirit of truth that one can discern clearly. 

Paul end the letter by letting them know that Tychicus will pay them a visit to give them an update of his status so that they could make some preparation to host him soon. In those times there wasn’t any booking apps that we can use like now but thru letters and so they have to make it ready at all times in case the guest arrive. In summary, the whole letter of Ephesians is for Paul to guide the Christians on their everyday lives, how to behave and it’s not to control them but to teach them the fundamentals of living a life in love for the intention is to live a life in loving God and our neighbours as ourselves.
