The letter to Colossians’ main purpose is to forewarn the church in Colossae founded by Epaphras with the gospel of our Lord Jesus Christ. He heard the gospel preached by Paul in Ephesus and Epaphras brought the gospel back to his hometown. He believed in the gospel and so did the Colossians which mostly consist of gentiles. However the society in Colossae were heavily influenced by human philosophy included Jewish customs, ascetic rules, visions, angelic or cosmic power and religious traditions. 

Paul wrote to them to affirm that the only thing needed for their salvation is their faith in Christ provided that they continue securely established and steadfast in the faith without shifting from the hope promised by the gospel of Christ that they have heard. Paul was chosen as a vessel from God to not only preach the gospel of Christ but also to correct false teachings and warn the followers of Christ against it. It is common for people to want to pursue knowledge and wisdom and the Colossians were not exempted, but Paul’s reminder is to lead them back to Christ.  

For example, a few years ago during the COVID lockdowns, churches were closed due to government’s decree as social distancing was introduced to cope with the plague. There were an explosion of online schools in all areas including Christianity, where I took the bait of those mystery of wanting to know God’s will thru visions and dreams. It was a hard lesson that I do not wish on anyone, alas what was done cannot be undone, I tried to warn others when I knew the truth but already they took measures to exile me from that group and slandered me in all areas of their connections. Lost my best friend and best partner in Christ, lost all trust in humanity and leaders of churches for many of them are wolves in sheep’s clothing. 

It wasn’t all bad though, because that’s what motivated me to pursue the truth in Christ even more, it was what started my obsession to study the bible and thru that I understand a little bit more though not all about God’s will. It was already written in the Holy Bible, it was always right there in front of my eyes but I was ignorant and was on the hype train until I jumped off the train and fell off the trail that I got back to the right track. God is amazing & I am so grateful for the teachings of Christ thru Paul as it resonated so much that it brought much comfort knowing that these battles were not mine alone. 

May our Lord Jesus Christ helps us all to stay on this true foundation of the gospel of Christ and continue to be steadfast in our beliefs in Him and reveals the truth to all creations.
