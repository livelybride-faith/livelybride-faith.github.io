Paul disclosed his intention here in chapter 3 why he sent Timothy to them, as he was worried because of the persecutions they faced and were beginning to doubt the timing of the return of our Lord Jesus Christ, they were also facing issues with sexual ethics and social relationships. Hence Paul was afraid that they might fall away from their faith and thus sent Timothy to them to strengthen and encourage them. 

It was not that bad yet but there’s another letter later so we know that from this point Paul is still very much relieved by the reports he got from Timothy to the point that he was grateful and even be encouraged about them through their faith. This is quite true in real life as well, in ministry, often times we face sufferings and dark times in the world, but when we heard about someone that we’ve ministered to holding on their faith as they too face the same or similar situations, somehow it strengthened us inside to be able to go thru day by day knowing that we all shared the same burden in our faith. This journey was never about walking alone, even though sometimes it is true that we are isolated no matter what the circumstances are, God is with us and the most amazing thing is with a heartfelt prayer in humility, God will hear and find a way to answer us. When we get that answer from God in one way or another, no matter from how or who or what, dreams, visions or reality, we know that we know it is from God because of the peace, love, comfort and hope that from those moments. It helps us to know that we are not truly alone and that’s what destroyed the stronghold of deception that Satan kept on wanting us to believe in. 

May our Lord Jesus Christ help us and strengthen our hearts in holiness whether in good days or bad days, in comfort or in distress that we may be blameless before our God and Father at the coming of our Lord Jesus with all His saints.
