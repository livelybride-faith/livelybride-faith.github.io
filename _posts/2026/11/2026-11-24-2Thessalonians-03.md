Paul believed in the power of prayer and he understood that God answered prayers of the righteous too not that God is constraint by it, but this is in the matter of context where Paul wanted the will of God to come to pass. He wanted that the word of the Lord may spread rapidly and be glorified everywhere. However there’s one more important point why he asked for prayer, it was so that they may be rescued from the wicked and evil ones that tortured them because he knew that not all of them have faith. His concern ultimately was still for others in the matters of faith. 

As the part for a warning from Paul against being jobless, I must emphasize on context here, this is about those who abuse the grace of God and feeling entitled to not working. This is not about those who have been trying hard to find a job and still cannot get it because of the economy and technology upheaval in this era now. This is also not about those who are still recovering from mental illness or depression or going thru spiritual warfare. It is wrong to use this passage to punish those who are struggling after all that they have been through but let us be wise in judgement and discernment base on righteous judgement and not by appearance. If one has not walk in one’s shoes, try observing and pray for them. 

It is also true and indeed a fact that some people are parasites, these are the people that Paul warned to keep away from them. We often see this among those who have much to give… unfortunately and this was what cause those who are wealthy to be cautious against who are not wealthy. That doesn’t mean that all those giving were in vain, if you lend to the poor, you are lending to God and God will reward you. One of the ways to still keep that giving spirit is to do our own research and pray for God to lead us and guide us in stewarding the resources that’s been given to us. Start with a good orphanage house and investigate first before giving because there are truly needed orphans who benefits from that but please do not give to those who abuse them. I pray that those abusers that still won’t repent experience the wrath of God and for God to rescue the orphans. Back to the letter, so Paul encouraged the believers to setup boundaries amongst the brothers and sisters who feels entitled and giving troubles by being a busybody in people’s lives so that they may be ashamed but not regard them as enemies. Confront them and hope that they will change, but if not then stay away from them. 

May our Lord Jesus Christ help us to be able to live a life worthy of the Gospel of Christ and give us peace at all times in all ways, may the Lord be with all of us.
