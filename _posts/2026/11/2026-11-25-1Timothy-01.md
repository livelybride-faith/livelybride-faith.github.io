Paul wrote the letters to Timothy whom he consider as his true son in faith as he was near death but there was much to be done to confront the church in Ephesus as they were facing severe crisis in the faith from the false teachings that led the believers away from the truth. His advice for Timothy is to instruct the church in Ephesus with love that comes from a pure heart, a good conscience and sincere faith. 

Some of the false teachers did not understand truly the purpose of the law which were created to protect the weak and against those who opposed all godly behaviours. Paul did not use the tone of entitlement or totalitarian but as someone who had also been thru a phase of actually being a false teacher in the past before he was transformed by Christ. He persecuted the church without truly understanding the laws and its purposes. He was acting in ignorance which means he truly believed what he was doing was godly and did not know that he was going against God. That’s why he said that he received mercy. He changed and transformed after he experienced the encounters with our Lord Jesus Christ on the road to Damascus meaning he truly repented and we have the privilege to read it from the Holy Bible. However there are some false teachers that insisted on false teachings and refused to give it up because usually it’s about control and manipulation to get what they desire that benefits themselves. Paul did not hesitate at all to turn those who rejected the conscience, meaning the faith and the truth over to Satan for the reason that they may be taught not to blaspheme. If they repented, I’d imagine that Paul will take them back, cautiously with boundary as well just as he had written in the Ephesians. As for those who did not truly repent, it was advised that they stay away from them, no longer in fellowship with them.  

So Timothy had a tough assignment to confront all these false teachers and prophets at a young age and he needed much encouragement in order to do that. Paul as a fatherly figure in faith, taught him what to do and strengthen him so that he could stand firm in his mission for the sake of the gospel of Christ. Sometimes, as believers we are in danger of being influenced by many false teachings, this is why we need to read and study the bible on our own as well as following legitimate sources. As for some young ministers, may this be an encouragement after all the rejections and slanders that you’ve taken for the sake of love in Christ.
