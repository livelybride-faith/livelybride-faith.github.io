title: 1 Thessalonians 5 - Rejoice, Pray, Give thanks

Paul continued with reminders for the Thessalonians that it is a certainly true that the day of the Lord will come but we do not know when. The analogy of come like a thief in the night is to emphasize that nobody knows the exact time when the Lord will come back again. 

It is also a reminder for all believers so that we do not fall into deception of false prophecies regarding the date, as in the past there has been cases where false leaders led people to a cult and then they took their own lives in mass numbers. Even so, Paul still encouraged the believers to not despise prophecies but test everything and hold fast to what is good. Sometimes deception from the church leaders destroy the whole trust towards humanity and Christianity but there’s a choice one can make wisely, it’s to test everything and hold fast to what is true, one of it is the salvation that has been given freely to all mankind by our Father in Heaven thru our Lord Jesus Christ. 

However this does not mean that believers should live life casually or ignorantly but to be watchful and in self-control so that we practice what Jesus Christ has taught us. This is a lifelong habit and not just a short term project, that we read and study the Bible all our days, to meditate God’s words day and night so that we do not forget God’s ways which leads to life. For the ways of evil, wickedness and sins leads to death. So Paul urged the Thessalonians to encourage and build up each other which they were already doing but as the circumstances of the persecution became worse, he wanted to acknowledge them and affirm them in their faith so that they do not despair. 

May our Lord Jesus Christ bless the believers in this community to rejoice always, pray without ceasing and give thanks in all circumstances, for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus for us. May the God of peace himself sanctify us entirely, and may our spirit and soul and body be kept sound and blameless at the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ.
