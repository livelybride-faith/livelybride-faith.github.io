Although Paul had never seen them face to face but thru Epaphras, he heard of their faith and wasn’t reluctant to show his affection in his letter to them. He maybe absent in body but he is with them in spirit, it is like when your children goes away for college and they introduce you to some new friends that had help them, the connection is formed due to the closeness that one had with the children. Epaphras was like Paul’s children and had formed the connection thru the introduction and description from him to Paul. 

Usually the parent will treat the friends of their children like their children in a protective mode when it comes to any kind of danger. That’s what Paul was expressing as it came to him naturally, zealous for God, instinctively protective of the gospel of Christ. So he warned them to beware of the false teachings such as needing extra steps to be saved for example circumcision, food law, festival observations, new moons or Sabbaths, self-abasement. There’s nothing else that one need other than believing in Christ as their Saviour and Lord, that Jesus Christ is the Son of God, He is also God, sacrificed Himself of the sins of humankind being sinless Himself and God caused Him to resurrected on the third day. Our Lord Jesus Christ now is reigning with God in the heavenly throne. The false teachings involved of worship of angels, initiatory visions still holding on to the elemental principles of the world such as ceremonialism in meats, drinks, washings, Essenic asceticism, pagan symbolism mysteries and rites. This is the context of what Paul was building his letter on. 

He did not want the followers of Christ to feel that they have been disqualified because they could not fulfilled all those elementary principles. That is the falsehood that Paul was battling against in the early church establishment. Those people think that they were being piety but they offered no value in checking self-indulgence in the flesh. Again, this is not to mocked them but this is to call them out so that they can be awakened to turn away from the deception and back to righteous path.
