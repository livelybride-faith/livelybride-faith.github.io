Paul commended the Christians in Thessalonica that they indeed lived a life pleasing to God and urge them to do so more and more especially in the area of physical holiness which is to refrain from committing sexual immorality. There’s a sign that when someone is filled with the Holy Spirit is when they experience being taught by God as in their heart feels the conviction from the Holy Spirit to not want to sin against God. One may failed when struggling and fall into sins but it is possible to reach the holiness of wanting to please only God and not the bodily desires. 

Now this is not an overnight victory unless one is blessed with that from God but I realized too that when I myself is committed with a goal with the help from the Holy Spirit that I can reach that goal not by myself but help from God and from people around us. Commitment requires planning as well to try and change the lifestyle by introducing friction around a temptation, just like those red tapes that surrounds financial institutions and laws that involve life and death decisions. Those boundaries are there to protect and not to cause harm. Then the next step is to introduce convenience into the habits that one wants to build, such as using automations to play the audio bible during driving or exercising or doing house chores by leveraging the technology available around us. Using safe apps or extensions on browsers to block profanities or NSFW images or addictive sites is also another way to guard our eyes from being tempted as our hearts will follow our eyes or ears mostly. 

Apart from that, just like any parent, it is pleasing to God that when their children lives in harmony with each other filled with love. I too feel that all people wants that but it is unfortunately in fact that this is quite hard to achieve because of much injustice that happens around us. So what do we do then, just like what we are doing now trying our best to do what we can to not touch the boundary that we know that has been setup by God or our parents. Usually greed is the root cause of everything surrounding injustice which then cause the victim to retaliate and then being accused of unreasonable because for a long time, they suffer in silence until they can no longer take it. It doesn’t excuse the hurt by the outburst of anger or rage however as a minister one must know how to discern and judge righteously instead of by appearance. This is in the context of someone who is in long term traumatic environment and does not excuse violence or abusive cases. 

Paul then reminded the truth and teachings about the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ, we as believers believed that He died on the cross for our sins and rose again, so those who have died before us, God will bring them to Him through Jesus Christ. When that day comes, imagine that today is the day, and we are still alive, we will be caught up in the clouds together with those who have died before us to meet the Lord in the air and be with Him forever. This is a longing for those whose heart has been captivated by our Lord Jesus Christ and nothing else on earth can satisfy that longing except being with God to be finally home in God. May that day be quicken.
