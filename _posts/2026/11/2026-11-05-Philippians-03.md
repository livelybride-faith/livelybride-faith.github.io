title: Philippians 3 - Press on toward the goal
tag: Ephesians

This chapter revealed that Paul was very much concern of the false teachings where one must be circumcised in order to be saved that might affect the church in Philippi. Then Paul proceed to tell them of his past where he was zealous persecutor of the church before he was transformed by Christ. Although he was very much qualified in terms of circumcision in the flesh and status within the Jewish sect, he exhorted the Philippians to forget about all that because it was based on work of men. Salvation can only be attain by believing in Christ thru faith, for it is given freely by grace from God. 

So he told the readers to not only stop at believing Christ, but to press on by forgetting what lies behind and straining forward to what lies ahead, the ultimate goal is to fulfill the will of God and live a life worthy of the gospel of Christ until we meet our Maker. There’s an afterlife where our Lord Jesus Christ will come the second time and there will be new heaven and new earth. This is a sign of maturity that a Christian has reach if they could focus on that ultimate goal. It’s not easy because we still live on this earth and there are many distractions that will pull away our attention from Christ and from the goal, sometimes we may even be stagnant or seems like we’re moving backwards but that’s why the daily bible reading is helpful, to remind us and pull back our attention towards Christ and the ultimate goal again. 

Paul encouraged the readers to imitate him instead of imitating the false teachers that seems powerful or religious in their daily life. It’s important to examine the fruit and not only by appearance. Let us not set our mind on earthly things but be mindful that we are indeed citizen of heaven and we ought to live in that way. May our Lord Jesus Christ empower us to live a life worthy of His gospel.
