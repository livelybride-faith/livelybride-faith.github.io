Paul urged the believers in Colossians to set their minds on things above, it doesn’t mean that trying to peek into the heaven to see what’s there, but the key is this, to behave and produce the fruit of compassion, kindness, humility, meekness and patience. In order to reach that, there are steps before that, which is to stop doing things that were listed such as sexual immorality, impurity, passion (lust), evil desire and greed (covetousness), anger that last longer, wrath - anger that boils over, malice, slander,  abusive speech and lying. 

There is another point that’s most important which is in this new clothing which means new transformation in Christ, there’s no longer division in between Greek and Jew, circumcised and uncircumcised, barbarian, Scythian, enslaved and free, but all are equal in Christ. This is to remind the readers that there’s no need to strive against each other and try to follow each other’s traditions in order to attain a superior status or position, nor there is a need to compare in between Jews and Greeks or among races as in Christ, all is the same. All that believe in Christ are saved. 

This is basically a repetition teaching from Paul’s common dictionaries & knowledge as we have read it in other letters as well, and here it repeats again, that wives be subject to husbands AS IS FITTING in the LORD, husbands, love your wives and NEVER TREAT THEM HARSHLY. There’s a condition for wives to be subject to husbands, what does it mean by as is fitting in the Lord? Think about that, it is not to be subject to them blindly even when they harm you or your children, in context of abusive cases. As for the husband, that verse is as clear as day. As for children, it’s important to obey parents as they always think for the best and always worries but maybe imperfect, they love their children nonetheless, but there are cases where parents abused their child, so that’s why Paul also mentioned Fathers, do not provoke your children. 

See, all these are responses with conditions, Paul’s letters should be freeing instead of enslaving for those who read it in a righteous mindset because it finally allow those who are under abusive situations to be free at last. As for forgiveness, the condition is this “forgive each other; just as the Lord has forgiven you, so you also must forgive”. Jesus Christ forgave and said “go, and sin no more” so repentance and reconciliation can only be reached if the perpetrator truly sin no more. The most hopeful verses in this chapter are these “since you know that from the Lord you will receive the inheritance as your reward; you serve the Lord Christ.* 25 For the wrongdoer will be paid back for whatever wrong has been done, and there is no partiality”.

The most important key in this chapter by Paul is this “clothe yourselves with love” and “whatever you do, in word or deed, do everything in the name of the Lord Jesus, giving thanks to God the Father through him”. May our Lord Jesus Christ help us all to grow and to be able to live a life worthy of the gospel and transform us according to His will.
