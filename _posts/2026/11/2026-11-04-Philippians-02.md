Paul urged the Philippians to live in harmony with each other in a society to serve each other in humility and of love, to regard each other as better than themselves not in a comparison or striving way but to acknowledge that each person is valuable as Jesus Christ sacrificed for each of us. It’s the hope of Paul for the Philippians just like any parent to their children to obey the teachings not only in their presence but also without because they want the best for them. 

Paul illustrated himself as being poured out as a libation over the sacrifice and the service of their faith but with joy because he did it for Christ and in the hope that they too will follow his footsteps which Paul himself followed our Lord Jesus Christ’s footsteps. Paul was always zealous for God but in the wrong way and when his eyes were opened during the event in the road to Damascus, where he encountered our Lord Jesus Christ, his zealousness did not fade away but it was redirected towards God’s will in his case, planting churches to expand the gospel of Christ. For those who are called and responded to Christ, it’s become their mission and goal to accomplish the will of God and we can see throughout the journey of Paul, sometimes he became impatient but in this chapter when he’s imprisoned and there’s not much places that he can roam about, he’s able to express his concern and care in a way that can touch people’s heart. There was not much action or activity except to be in a stationary place which was not a bad thing as his works became beneficial to believers until now.
 
It’s an encouragement for us believers who maybe in constraint or feel constraint due to the circumstances that we are in but from another perspective, it gives us much space and time to be able to connect with God more such as reading the bible, studying it, spending time with Christian fellowship.
