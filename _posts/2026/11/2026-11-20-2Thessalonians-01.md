The second letter from Paul to the Thessalonians was probably after a few months, and it was to address some rising issues from the church in Thessalonica. Particularly the Day of the Lord, some Christians stopped working and became busy bodies interfering in others’ lives, it must have been quite serious. This is quite an eye opener that even in that era, there were such cases as of now in 2026 as well. Some people use their mouth more than their hands and we’ve all seen it in the church, family and workplace. This is in the context of those who always complained but never helps and no matter what was done to improve it, they are still picky about many things. I’m not saying that this is the case in Thessalonica but it could be just one of the example. 

The first main issue during the time of the letter being written was about the Day of the Lord, the second coming of our Lord Jesus Christ. It is not uncommon as the mind easily succumb to the influence of deceptions and try to find an easier or more convenient way to reach a conclusion. How else can one explain that we often find the laziest way to accomplish something. Now with AI, people want frictionless and everything to just works. It’s become dangerous and need some reminders that there will always be things or plans that do not work as it is and we have to workarounds. This is in the context of some problems that we need to solve and not about criticizing or saying that frictionless flow is not a good thing. It is to say that inconvenience is not a bad thing as it requires problem solving skill and that will increase critical thinking skill. 

The danger is that it can pamper a human being to become such a person that could not take no for an answer or when things don’t go their way, they could not handle it. So not everything is about frictionless and this is in the context of one why it’s important to know the truth in a whole and not just part of it. For example, there were some cults in the past that predicted which day is the end of the world and they influenced others to give up on living. Some of those who were already burdened with troubling issues in life will just follow easily because they have tried much and life didn’t work out the way they wanted so they chose the easy way out which is to believe the lies fed to them. This is just how some people cope not to criticized them but it is a fact that human being do act like that. 

So Paul’s reminder was vital to the church before it escalated into something horrible. First, he acknowledged their faith and affirmed them that he was proud of them and to tell them that they indeed have been growing. This gives some hints about their state of mind at that time as persecution and afflictions had increased among them and that might be one of the reason that they started to believe in the lies. He assured them that indeed those who do not believe in the gospel of our Lord Jesus Christ will suffer the punishment of eternal destruction, separated from the presence of the Lord and from the glory of His might. This acts as a reminder to them and Paul prayed for them out of concern so that they can live a life worthy of the gospel. 

May our Lord Jesus Christ reveal the truth and strengthen us in our inner being, help us to think clearly especially in the time where artificial intelligence is affecting the whole world and keep us ground in His truth, let us not despair in our situation or circumstances but to help us to live a life worthy of Him.
