title: 1 Thessalonians - Joy in persecution for hope is in Christ

Paul was someone that walked the talk as he went thru persecutions and sufferings in Philippi which was recorded in the book of Acts chapter 17 for the sake of the gospel of Christ. So he truly understood the situation of the Thessalonians and he commended them that it is good and glorious of what they have gone thru as well for the sake of the truth. It is a common thing for people to bond easily when they have some sort of similar experience in life because people crave understanding and it enhanced the sense of belonging or not alone which somehow feels comforting. 

Paul’s words in the letter might have given them permission to be able to feel seen and understood, as if God truly knows what they were going thru. This is vital in the journey of faith because of the acknowledgement of peers or other people even more so from someone like Paul to commend them, so that one may have hope and be strengthened in persecution and do not fall in to despair or prolonged bitterness. Paul emphasized that he did not use flattery or any kind of bait to lure them in order that he may gain something from them. He mentioned that he cared for them deeply and his concerns towards them was like a nurse tenderly caring for her own children. This paint a picture of how self-sacrificing to be life giving towards others. Paul was an example of a bi-vocational pastor in the early church era, as he worked and provided for himself so that he might not become a burden. He said this to remind them that although he had the privileges to demand support from them, he did not claimed it or feel entitled to it. 

He urged them to imitate him so that they too do not become entitled. Sometimes when suffering is too much or the difficulties in life feels too overwhelming, it will change a person’s mindset or twist them into thinking that they are entitled to feel or to demand anything because they feel wronged by someone in some part of their lives. When those demands were not met automatically, they feel offended or rejected and it become an obsession for them to demand justice or their so called “rights”. We can see this happening all around the world, in the church, in families & relationship, daily in news no matter the fields or types. Paul led them back to the truth and the ultimate goal, our Lord Jesus Christ. When Paul said that they, the Thessalonians were their glory and joy, he was actually reminding them that their suffering too will not be in vain and this will remove a huge part of bitterness or burden that comes from the suffering.
