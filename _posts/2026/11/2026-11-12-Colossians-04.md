The further instructions from Paul is devotion to prayer and be alert in thanksgiving because he knows very well how easy it is for believers to fall into the temptation of complaints, bitterness, slanders and depression. It doesn’t mean that believers will be exempt from all these sufferings and life will be glorious and happily ever after when we accept Jesus Christ as our Lord and Saviour. There are still spiritual battles to be fought or struggle upon where we battle in the position of victory but not quite there yet. 

Paul then taught the believers to behave wisely towards non believers, making the most of the time, this I believe is to treat them as God’s valuable creation in order that the gospel of Christ may touch their hearts and lives so that maybe one day they can know Christ too. Even when it’s not for this purpose, we ought to treat every human being respectfully and decently regardless of faith. If the only goal to be friends is to share the gospel then people will feel it too and it will deem as insincere, who then would want to maintain such a relationship. Most of Church communities has been like that hence when there’s no longer a common goal or mission in Christ, the relationship dies off, because it was never built on a foundation of love and genuine interests of being friends at heart. That’s why people feel that it’s fake and some even worse, it’s only for material gain, yes, money. So then, evangelize is not bad, but doing that from a genuine authentic heart of really loving the people we evangelize to goes a long way. It becomes an eternal relationship and not just a goal to be reached. Paul demonstrated exactly this as he spent time to convey his teachings, messages, concerns and expressed his emotions thru letters because he genuinely love them all as his family in Christ. 

The letter ended with greetings and benedictions, so that’s how we know this letter has been circulated amongst the early church during Paul’s time. Geographically Laodicea, Hierapolis and Colossae are quite near to each other. Specifically Colossae was only 11 miles SE of Laodicea and 15 miles S SE of Hierapolis. He gave credits and honored those who have been faithful in serving Christ and helping him when in need and even encouraged Archippus to complete the task that’s been received in the Lord. Sometimes believers need encouragement and acknowledgement will help them feel seen and appreciated. Although one mustn’t do anything from that intention of getting seen or being acknowledged, these kind words helps people to win the battles of negative thoughts that comes from the devil. 

In summary, the Colossians is a letter for Christians to be reminded of the truth, Jesus Christ is enough for our salvation and how Christians ought to live and behave now in journey of faith and to live a life worthy of the gospel of Christ.
