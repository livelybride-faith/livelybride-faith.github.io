The letter of Philippians was address to the gentile Church in Philippi which was established by Paul, the “An introduction to the Bible” mentioned that this is the first European church that Paul planted. It’s one of the church that brought Paul joy as we can all sense from his letter later on. He started the letter with much prayers of joy and meaningful deeply thoughtful words, one can almost sense his deep care and love in those words. It’s one of those light tone that can be differentiate from the previous letters we read which were full of rebukes and heaviness. 

As Paul feel much joy and favor towards the Philippians, he disclosed much of his emotions and heartfelt desires in his heart, letting the Philippians know that his desire is to depart and be with Christ but also longing to stay for them because it’s much more necessary for them. This is also an example of selflessness just like Christ. At this time, he was actually imprisoned because he cast out a demon from a girl and her master was enraged as it was used as a source of income to deceive others. Due to the circumstances, when he revealed that he was a Roman citizen, the magistrates had to apologize for the way that they treated him and that’s how it was actually a progress in the gospel that he spoke of. Though he felt that it was an utter torment to suffer in the flesh, he spoke of living is Christ and dying is gain. It happens to most of us believers, when suffering comes, we tend to want it all to be over and when it becomes too much we know that nothing in this world is worth living for except for Christ. So we hold on to that hope in faith one more day, one step at a time relying on God for strength. 

Paul encouraged the Philippians to live a life in a manner worthy of the gospel of Christ, to be bold and courageous. Some background of the Philippians, they are mostly traders and one of the traits of traders are they don’t want much trouble but just to build life for a win, win, win situation for all parties if possible. Of course there are some that are far more greedy, but in this context, they were very generous in giving to Paul in financial support as well. So there maybe a lot of predators that wanted to prey on them and was jealous of Paul as well so it made sense that they were opposed and led to fear. So Paul comforted them and exhorted them leading them back to the gospel of Christ with his own testimony. 

There are times when in our journey with Christ that we will meet with difficulties and sufferings but let today’s chapter be an encouragement for us believers that to live is Christ and to die is gain, for we live not for ourselves anymore and to live a life worthy of the gospel of Christ.
