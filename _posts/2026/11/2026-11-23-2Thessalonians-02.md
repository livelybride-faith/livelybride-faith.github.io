Reading this chapter is a bit terrifying but maybe that’s why there’s a saying can you handle the truth. The context of the matter that Paul was addressing is about the rumours that caused the Thessalonians to doubt what they have been taught which is our Lord Jesus Christ will come again. They thought that it already happened and if I would to assume or imagine what happened, it would be that someone tried to deceive or believed that they themselves were the Messiah. This has been happening throughout history in various masks and themes. Idolatry that compelled people to be worshipped or even to worship objects or any subjects is one of them. 

Back then without the convenience of internet or phone or fax, Paul used the route of physical letters delivery to convey his message and his heart. As for now, even though with the convenience of internet, with a press of button, it may seem easier but in truth it is harder for that message to reach the audience due to the noise and risking the truth being drown in the oceans of notifications. However I do believe in the power of the Holy Spirit, sometimes our eyes and our heart will be captivated to actually listen to a certain sentence or word. It lingers in our mind and heart for a while, pay attention and take notice of those moments then bring it to God and study the bible with it. God can use any channel or any kind of tools to speak to His children, the problem is, are we listening? I’m in awe of how God orchestrated or somehow caused that one point of a moment where the whole production took years and efforts of thousands of people to achieve, just to speak to one’s heart, to acknowledge, and to let the person feel seen. This is the amazing God that I believe in, the Creator that cause such miracles to happen, the One that could right all the wrongs. Not in a way that the history will be wiped, but in the path of redemption that one can be healed and move forward. 

Paul spoke the truth in that moment using letters and God is still using people to preserve all these written words so that believers will be able to read those letters and glean on it. The most terrifying thing is that when one is deceived and yet do not know it. However, as believers we can always pray, for our Lord Jesus Christ told us “Ask, and you shall receive”. It is known that James said that we ask and do not receive because we ask wrongly, in order to spend what we get on our pleasures (James 4:2-3). If we ask for the truth to be revealed, it is not only for our benefits but also for others and for God’s will to happen. God will answer always, mercifully in some occasions. That was what happened when the truth was revealed regarding the cultish so called online elite schools of prophets. Begging and pleading for truth daily while living in fear because of the grooming and conditioning of mass in the group. Lord Jesus Christ is my Saviour even from false teachings within Christianity. I confess that He is my Lord when I accepted Him without much understanding, today after what I’ve been thru, I confessed with much conviction that Lord Jesus Christ is my Lord and Saviour and He is worthy of it all. If God answered and has already presented the truth but people refused to believe it, continuing in their wickedness and falsehood then they will perish and be condemned. So there’s a choice that one has to make, to choose truth over falsehood. 

May our Lord Jesus Christ Himself and God our Father, who loved us and through grace gave us eternal comfort and good hope, comfort our hearts and strengthen us in every good work and word.
