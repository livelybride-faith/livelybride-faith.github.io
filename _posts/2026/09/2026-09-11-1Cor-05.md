Paul begun to directly address the issue of sexual immorality amongst the Christians in Corinth, which he described was not found even in the gentiles, a man is living with his father’s wife. The original root word in Greek for “living” is ἔχω, with various meaning of to have, possess, a relationship, to occupy, dwell in, in other words the man was having an intimate sexual relationship with his father’s wife, either a biological mother or step mother. Either way, it was sexually immoral. 

The only way is to reprimand this person in order that he repent and stop the relationship entirely but if not then send him out of the church or the Christian community in order that he may realized the shame in isolation that this is truly anti social and sinful. This only applies to the brothers and sisters in faith, meaning believers and Christians. Those who are unbelievers and pagans, we do not judge them that way for that is for God to judge. We are not to isolate ourselves from the world for we are the light and the salt that can share the gospel and be able to serve God by any help that we can provide amongst them. It doesn’t mean that we build intimate relationship with them. It’s just to bring out the meaning of we do not stay in a bubble full of Christians and refuse to go out into the world but to follow the teachings of our Lord Jesus Christ, share the gospel, heal the sick, cast out demons. The intention to excommunicate a person who refuse to repent is so that it doesn’t influence or affect others in the community. 

Paul explained clearly that his letter is not to associate with anyone who call themselves Christians but did not repent nor feel any remorse at all or in other words, they feel that they are always right and justify their actions with certain excuses arrogantly without any sign of wanting to repent. The type of Christians that were listed are those who commit idolatry, greedy, sexually immoral, a reviler (slanderer, abusive, taunt, treat disrespectfully, insult, curse either God or human being), drunkard or swindler. He commanded do not even eat with such a one. You will know by their fruits and how they speak, as you confront them over and over again, they will use your words against you and counter back with gaslighting leaving you feel guilty by turning the whole situation around by victimizing themselves when they were the one that is guilty. Then you will be able to discern also who is genuinely struggling to repent and is following the instructions but really need help because of addiction or spiritual stronghold. He said God will judge unbelievers, but we as Christians must drive out the wicked person from among us. 

The principle is this, that we practice judgement among believers not by appearance but by righteous judgement which means that we have relationship with the believers and we know their story so that we can speak to them in love, and if they refuse to repent and continue to sin with arrogance then we’ve done what we can so we must cut them off from our lives. However there’s still hope because Paul said hard this person over to Satan for the destruction of the flesh, so that the spirit may be saved in the day of the Lord. Though it is a final resort but it is not all at loss. This applies to both gender, man and woman hence it is a principle and not a rigid rule by picking the exact wording out of a sentence of context. As a reminder, in this chapter, the particular context and case is a man committed sexual immorality with his father’s wife. Keep in mind, that there are some cases that a victim of sexual abuse in this case does not count, for it was the perpetrator that cause that and often times the victims were groomed from a very young age. 

May our Lord Jesus Christ help us all to continue to race together in order that we may reach that goal that God has set for us, help us to be righteous discerners and judge, help us to also stay humble and merciful, help us and give us strength to reach the repentance and His will.
