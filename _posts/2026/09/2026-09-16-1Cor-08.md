Paul addressed the issue of food offered to the idols, is it lawful or unlawful to eat? The short answer is, it doesn’t matter as long as it doesn’t affect anyone’s conscience. Hence the easiest way is to not eat for the sake of others and there’s a decent way to reject it according to the audience. 

For example, if idolaters who have just prayed to the idols with the food, their belief is shown in that action that the food were “blessed”, to us as Christian, we know that there’s only one true God and there’s no such belief. It’s based on their conscience, and we ought to respect the conscience of other believers as well for food offered to idol is based on “faith”. We put our faith in Christ and they put their faith in idols. Those who went thru upbringing of such culture may sometimes carry over the beliefs into their Christian faith and because they believe that they have sin against God then they have truly sin against God because doing what they think that have sin against God  already deemed them guilty. 

This is the matter of conscience in context. Which is why Paul urged the church in Corinth to understand from this point of view not for self but for others. He wanted to explain that this is about love, building others up. It’s much deeper than just following the rules, but it’s about being sensitive and conscious about other’s wellbeing in terms of feelings, emotions, conscience and their spiritual growth. It is another level of how a Christian should learn to think and take notice on, will my action right now cause others to stumble in their faith or spiritual growth? It is indeed a principle, not just regarding food offered to idols. It applies to a wide range of activities, for example in Africa, there was a history of Christendom conversion in the land and the missionaries in order to eliminate drunkeness, they conveyed that it is a sin to drink alcohol. This is imprinted to many Christians in that land, so it’s important when we interact with others from other cultures and to not cause any stumbling in their faith. So, it is clear, this is not about us, self, but it’s a community matter, if we choose to live in a community then we must be responsible and maintain the social harmony in a society. So it is not just about food. 

Hence Paul said that if food is a cause of their falling, he will never against eat meat, so that he may not cause one of them to fall. Meat is delicious and nutritious, but Paul was willing to give up meat for others’ sake. This is the main point of this part of the letter. He wants to bring out the importance of how Christians should carry out their liberty such as eating in the temple of an idol publicly. It is for the sake of others, it is for the sake of love for building up others.
