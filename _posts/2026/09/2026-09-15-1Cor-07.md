Paul gave some very specific directions regarding marriage but the principle is always about pleasing God. His instructions was specific to those who are unmarried that are not practicing self-control to the point of committing sins of sexual immorality. For those who have passion of desire, it is better to marry but then again even so, their body is for the purpose of serving each others’ needs and so it still all rooted of being unselfish and wanting to fulfill the spouse’s desires in terms of sexual desire. It’s both ways and that will lead to a good intimate relationship in the marriage. Intimacy thru physical contact and sexual fulfillment is vital for a healthy marriage aside from communications and how each one treat one another. 

Those who are married according to God, Paul gave command to not divorce, again see the context in this chapter, read the next few verses, to the rest Paul said, not from God, if anyone who is unbeliever and then converted to become a Christian, and if their spouse does not believe but is still willing to live together, meaning that both must make compromises then they should not divorce. For who knows then, their love towards each other by the mercy of God that when they see each other’s treatment may have convince them that the gospel that they preach is true? However that may need the believer to actually walk the talk. So the context is about a believer and unbeliever. So if in a marriage, there are abuses that has been going on for a while even after seeking counsel and there’s no change, please for your own sake and your children sake, even for that partner sake, take drastic measure to separate before it becomes a crime. 

Now, he touched the core of the message, all these arguments that he had to addressed specifically were because it was happening in the church of Corinth, that is the argument and false teachings of needing circumcision to be saved or feel more superior or holier than others. Paul said circumcision is nothing, and let all who were already circumcised and those who were not, remain as is, and for those who were slaves also do not be concerned of it for they are free belonging to the Lord. The point is, as long as one is following God in faith and not just to check every box of rules and regulations. 

For those who are virgins, Paul did not have any specific rules or commands but instead he gave advices as someone who experienced life. Given his background, as a Pharisee, and many may have seek his advices in synagogues before his transformation in Christ, he must have seen a fair share of marriage dramas. Though, it is not sinful to marry nor to remain single, he did mentioned that those who marry will experience distress in the flesh. We all know that marriage is not easy and it needs a lot of effort, compromise and sacrifices to have at the least a healthy one at baseline. First, the time for friends, hobbies and self will be stripped by a big chunk. Unless both happens to enjoy doing the same hobbies together, but sometimes people change their hobbies too. Hence Paul said the married will be anxious about the world and seek how to please their spouse, that much is true. However it is not a restraint, and I believe in happy marriage as well for children is a blessing from God. A blessing that none can give, so the bigger the blessing the bigger the effort of course. 

May our Lord Jesus Christ bless us all with happy marriage and also be able to please Him together in a marriage serving God with much abundance and blessings, may our Lord Jesus Christ also bless us all with children and help us to raise them as Godly children. May our Lord Jesus Christ also bless us who are single or widowed to be content and fulfilled and joyful as we serve Him in different ways.
