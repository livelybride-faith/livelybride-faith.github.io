This chapter is continuing from Paul’s advices regarding the issue of to not become a stumbling block to others in the journey of spiritual growth in Christ. The fact that Paul had to write and give specific explanation regarding the headship or source of man, woman and God with the paragraph ending with “if anyone is dispose to be contentious, we have no such custom, nor do the churches of God”. Paul spoke to the male person that a female person is the glory of the male person, which he was trying to affirm the status of a female person. Then Paul spoke to the female person explaining that a male person is the source due to the creation order where a male was created first then the female out of the male, which then only they were able to manage the land, animals and offsprings. Sometimes people are so fixated about the specific rule and traditions that it became a natural contentious issue arising without even know why. Paul knew, it was always about striving against each other. 

Clearly some weird questions arose because of the need to affirm each other’s point of view of who is right and wrong, or what should be done and not be done. It all rooted out from striving and wanting the benefits for self. So all these would have not been an issue if there’s no judgement against each other that who is better or more superior, once that argument starts, it will only create havocs. Paul was simply saying, without God there would be no Christ, without Christ there would be no male person, without male person there would be no female person, without female person, there would be no glory to the male person, each male and female person should not even try to compete or compare with each other, just serve God in whatever ways one can willingly. If a man can pray faithfully then let him pray, if a woman can pray faithfully then let her pray, it wasn’t about restraining their freedom but about giving up own self for the benefits of others. In simple words, the source is our God, our Creator, our Maker; all things come from God. 

The matters of contention obviously carried over to holy communion that involved eating and drinking where people were ignoring those who were less well off and in need. They were not sharing portions proportionately that some had to go hungry and the Lords’ supper was always about giving to those who needs. Our Lord Jesus Christ said, this is my body, broken, and gave it to the disciples, the cup which represents His blood shed on the cross for our forgiveness, passing it and dividing to the others. Now imagine the scene, what if some people drank it all up. Maybe they did brought more food or drink but what if they felt others unworthy of the resources that they provided? That would defeat the whole purpose of the holy communion for our Lord Jesus Christ demonstrated sharing with others not withholding. So again, there was a lot of striving and contention in the church of Corinth and Paul was trying to address the issue.

May our Lord Jesus Christ help us to be humble and realize that God is the source of everything, that there’s no point in striving for it does not add anything at all. Let us examine our hearts that though we are different from each other but it doesn’t mean who is better or worse in terms of superiority or any kind of measurements for we all comes from God.
