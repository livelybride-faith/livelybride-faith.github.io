Paul urged the Christian community in Rome to tolerate each other’s weakness in faith not in the meaning of humiliating them with the word “failing of the weak” but to bring out the point that those who are already firm and matured in a certain level in faith where the religious rules outside of moral necessity does not affect their conscience anymore should be more understanding towards those who are still growing spiritually. In simple terms, if one believe that they are sinning against God and still do it, then they have sinned already. This only applies to matters outside of moral scope or does not involved anything that involve righteousness and justice lest this be twisted.

For example, if one commit adultery and said it is for love, that is still sinning because it roots out of greed where in the first place one already made a vow and a marriage covenant with their wive or husband. Even in abusive relationship, one should go thru a proper procedure of communication and counselling in the hope of salvage and reconciliation but this can only work when both parties are willing to put effort into it. If it’s too late for that then the damage and trust has already been done where the next step would be divorce. All these in fact only need some common sense and should not be needed to be told like a black and white rules of conditions like a software programmer. Anyone that does programming will understand that for the applications does not have a mind and cannot think, it can only do what it’s asked, hence there’s a lot of if else conditions and sometimes conflict of interest will crash the app because it doesn’t know what to do. Human has a mind, a brain that can process thoughts, it is meant to be use to think logically, then human has heart too, that can feel emotions and judge accordingly. So when people try to find a loophole in the moral laws and God’s commandments, they are acting like a machine without heart or mind.  They became selfish just to fulfilled their own desires. Here Paul tries to bring the readers back to mind that each of us exist not to please ourselves but for the purpose of building up our neighbors. For Christ did not come to be served, but to serve. 

Paul knew that after reading the letter that he sent, it may feel heavy and harsh towards the Christian community in Rome, so he explained that he said all this as a reminder and believe that they are full of goodness, filled with all knowledge, and able to instruct one another, not to talk down on them but to just bring back to their memory that they too are of a people with a standard measure of certain qualities. It may seem patronizing but sometimes it is inevitable because people often forgets who they are in Christ due to flock mentality and influence hence he just wanted to remind them just like how we are taught to read the bible daily in order to remind us. May our Lord Jesus Christ help us to think clearly according to His will and to help us to encourage and build each other up.
