It seems to be that there were some lawsuits going on in the Corinth especially in the church that Paul addressed and it has something to do with defraud. If that was so, then the leaders of the church as they are all brothers and sisters should be able to investigate and find out what was wrong, then demand that the perpetrator to return plus compensate it. However maybe Paul had more information regarding the whole case. 

From what was written, it appeared to be that they were striving against each other to get back at each other, maybe the person who were wronged defraud the perpetrator and then it goes round and round until it become something so big to involve the authority but it could also be that the people at the church did not want to get involve in family matters. Paul then ask a question about why not rather be defrauded instead? This is the part that even I struggle to accept. For this type of enabling in the end in often lead to the perpetrator’s end without a turning point. Their heart was so dulled and ignorant because of all the enabling but maybe then this is their punishment that they deserve for all their wickedness. People shun away from them and they in the end was left feeling even more isolated and lonely to which either might push them to repentance or to the even more extreme that they create more harm. 

Ofcourse, one can pray to God for vindication, for justice, the Bible taught that to leave it to God for vengeance, it’s insane and inhuman to ask people to not feel pain and grieve or any emotion at all when one is wronged. We bring all that emotion to God, pray to Him for vindication, that shows that our hearts are still tender and want justice and righteousness. If our hearts do not hate the act of wickedness and evil, then we must ask ourselves have our hearts grown dull. Of course there are cases where they were diagnosed with depression or some emotional and mental illness and they are recovering from that, a sick person should not be judge with such standard. 

Then Paul moved forward to talk about sexual immorality, his argument principle is this that all things are permitted but not all things are beneficial. All things are permitted for me, but will not be dominated by anything. He was talking in plain language, such as we are allowed to drink alcohol, play games, smoke, perform sexual activities but the question is “is it beneficial?” The main issue here is prostitution, and he is warning them that this is a sin against the body itself for our body is a temple of the Holy Spirit within us. He encouraged the church of Corinth to glorify God in our body, that means whatever we do with our body matters and we are responsible for the consequences of our actions. Whoever is united to a prostitute becomes one body with her, for it is said the two shall be one flesh. So beware of sex outside marriage too, though Paul did not laid it out in clear wordings but in the next chapter, his words showed that. 

May our Lord Jesus Christ help us to glorify Him in body and in spirit and lead us away from temptation.
