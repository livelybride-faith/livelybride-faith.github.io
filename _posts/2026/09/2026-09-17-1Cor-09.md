In this chapter, Paul touched the subject about the eligibility of his apostleship in the church of Corinth, there may have been some accusations arose against him regarding his position as an apostle that required him to defend his stance. He did not shun away from providing a reasonable explanation but used the law of Moses that comes from God. He used a lot of rhetorical questions in a sarcastic manner so that people can be shaken enough to see his point. One manner of how he defended against the accusation was similar to how  our Lord Jesus Christ faced satan when He was tested in the wilderness, with the word of God but within context. 

The devil always likes to use the word of God out of context in order to accuse others but this one may need to truly understand a person whether they were really brainwashed by others’ teachings or that they really used it in order to benefit themselves. This is because new believers that join a church will follow blindly as their faith is fresh and eager to learn, so when they were led by false teachers, they will only be able to mirror and repeat what was taught. That was my experience in a church that was abusive and wanted only money and control over their members. This is why it is important to study the word of God ourselves, to understand the scriptures in context and asking God to reveal the truth to us. So here, Paul demonstrated on how to bring truth to light when he sensed that the church in Corinth began to be so twisted in their mind. 

On the other hand, it is a serious matter regarding livelihood of a person, God created such law to provide wages for the workers because it’s about the wellbeing of the whole society. A society thrives when everyone receive fair wages, that includes the workers for the gospel for they spent resources on books, education, time, energy for the sake of gospel. It’s the same as how economy can affect the crime rates and safety within a society. People may not resort to steal or rob if they have enough though this only applies to non greedy people. As usual, the world is not without evil and there will always be the outliers. So in this chapter, it is important to examine the fruits and give wisely according to God’s will, willingly, within our capacity. Even so, Paul did not exercise that right but worked as a tentmaker to support his own livelihood and ministry. It was a good thing because then he was able to not be influenced for it’s highly probably that one may be affected by those who “invested” on them and once a disagreement arise, the pressure to yield to the decision of the investor will mostly overtake the will to stand firm. 

This is a reminder that both believers or leaders of a church, that we ought to go right back to the root, why do we believe in God, why do we serve God? Was it not all for the sake of the gospel? Was it not to understand more about our Lord Jesus Christ? Was He not the one that save us all? There are many distractions that will pull our hearts away from God, I’m not speaking about merely attention or time or resources, but of the alignment of the heart, the agenda and motivation that drives our actions daily. It’s vital that we focus our heart and mind on our Lord Jesus Christ not because we want to brainwash ourselves into ignoring everything else but it’s to set that goal post right, then only our actions will follow. So how do we align our hearts? Read the word of God and study it extensively in terms of asking question like what does it really mean in this context? Why does it say what it says here? Was it do to with the culture back then? Did something happened? Learn to think thru all those questions and answers will come as you seek. 

May our Lord Jesus Christ help us all to run this race together and reach His goal for us, let us not stray from the heart of God to the right nor to the left and align our hearts and minds with God’s will, may He help us to be affirm in our legitimacy in Christ and put our trust and be secure in Him.
