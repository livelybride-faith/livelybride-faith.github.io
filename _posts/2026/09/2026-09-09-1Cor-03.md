title: 1 Cor 3 - All belongs to God
tag: 1Cor
---
Paul could not speak to the church in Corinth as spiritual people but as fleshly, infants in Christ. This is about the behavior and the level of maturity in a person’s walk with a community of faith where they still behave and react in the sense that they have to win, or feel superior over others, always striving against others, some may not realized that they are doing it for they has not yet gain that insight and maturity to be able to sense it. 

I speak as one who did strive against others without knowing that was striving, without knowing that I was afraid of losing, for many years I was blinded mainly due to the church that I was in was also striving against each other all the time. Their culture was performative and those who perform best without real transformation were rewarded and that was addicting. So all the sheep just followed thru and never think much deeper. It was in a mission trip that when I heard the voice of Christ to tell me urgently to follow Him, that I started to see and sense what was wrong with my walk in faith. After leaving that toxic environment, there’s guilt and post trauma to be dealt with and to get those out of the system. It took years and a lot of destroying strongholds and rebuilding the foundation in Christ by truly reading and studying the word of God. 

So yes, it is confirmed that even the church of God can sin this way by causing striving, dissension, division by just competing against each others. When this happens, conflict and division will be inevitable and people were force to choose a side. However now that we’re reading the letters of Paul, then we know that we don’t have to choose a side like this but always to be in Christ. In simple words, choose God. There’s no intention in slandering any church but it is a fact that people are often manipulated in a community and abusive leaders were often overlooked for the sake of love, but if one truly follows the word of God, one shall realize that the standard of a leader is always to serve and not to be served from the a pure intention of just want to serve God and not for self benefits. As a church of God, the leaders should understand this and that it is futile to think that anyone is more or less important, for all belongs to God and we are God’s coworkers, working together. The only foundation on any person that should be built is Jesus Christ. 

Paul warned sternly that the church in Corinth were God’s temple, the Spirit of God, which is the Holy Spirit dwells in them, they who destroy God’s temple will be destroyed by God. This is in the context of how the church of Corinth created division and conflict by striving against each other to cause people to say who they belong to except Christ. Isn’t that a fact? That throughout history, from orthodox, to catholic to protestants and so many more denominations, it kept on dividing and the whole body of Christ for they believe in Christ, right? were just in a loop of striving & debating against each other about doctrines created by men. Foolish. That’s what Paul said. I agree because when God opened my eyes, then I saw how foolish it was. 

Paul did not left it there, only a problem, he gave a solution, that is all things are the church’s whether Paul or Apollos or Cephas or the world or life or death or the present or the future, all are yours, and you are Christ’s and Christ is God’s. He led them all back to God. The you here in original language is plural, so he meant the church in Corinth but yes it can be applied to all church of God across all time and space. This is the truth, everything is God’s. This is how we come to a point of humility by realizing that all belongs to God then the heart can stop striving. May our Lord Jesus Christ help us all and open our eyes that we may see, then reach the spiritual maturity that God intends for us.
