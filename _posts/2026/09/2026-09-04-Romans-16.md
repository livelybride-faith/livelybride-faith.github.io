title: Romans 16 - Avoid dissensions and keep our Lord’s teachings
tag: Romans
---
Paul end the letter to the Romans with personal greetings and affection before a final instruction to be watchful and avoid those who create dissensions and hindrances, in opposition to what they have learned. The strength of a pure hearted person may also become the weakness if they are not careful with the people they mingle with for it’s easy to be influenced and fall into deception. As such people are trustful towards others by nature and willingly share and absorb whatever that’s around them. That is why one must be wise in choosing their friends and be led by God’s word instead of what people say. The solution is getting wisdom and insight from the Holy Bible in context, not just commentaries and podcasts. Those are good but it’s better to study the bible personally to really understand what it really means. 

Paul reminded the readers to always go back to what they have learned, in this context of the whole book of Romans, that is the salvation thru faith in Christ Jesus, our Lord not by works. Grace is freely given by God and cannot be earned. Our salvation is a gift from God thru our Lord Jesus Christ and it can never be attained thru works. We are not able to work or pay or sacrifice enough to be able to make the payment of salvation. That is why no one can boast of the works that was already accomplished by God. His intention is to urge the readers not to be conformed to the rules and regulations, such as doctrines of men in food and drink, but it doesn’t mean that we enforce that to others. He wants us to accept and tolerate each other in such matters for it doesn’t touch anything regarding righteousness and justice. It’s coming from a position of doing what’s beneficial to others in their spiritual growth. The whole context is to not debate or argue over such matters but keep the harmony and peace among believers. 

Of course, after we receive the gift of salvation, we cannot abuse the grace and gift by intentionally keep on sinning, but yes we do struggle as Paul did too, even the disciples of Christ struggle in the journey of faith. However we do not give up, we keep on relying on God to help us to reach the goal set by God. Sometimes we fall into temptations but it is not the end as long as we don’t give up, we come before God and ask for forgiveness then ask for strength to help us overcome the sin or give us a way out. 

God is our creator, our Maker, full of mercy and love, He wants to provide and protect His children, and by His Spirit, He will help us if we let Him. May our Lord Jesus Christ help us to serve in righteousness and love, to be able to rely on Him, to put our faith and trust in Him.
