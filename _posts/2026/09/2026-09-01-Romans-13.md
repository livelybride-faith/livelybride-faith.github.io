Now Paul touches the subject of government, he encouraged with such assurance that governing authorities that exist have been instituted by God. So those who resist the authorities resist God and they who resist will incur judgement. Originally the authorities were setup in order to enforce justice to protect the weak for we all know that some people are bullies and robbers, so they were suppose to uphold justice and to help keep the peace and harmony in a society. The logic is this, if we did not commit any crime or evil, there should be no reason to be afraid of authorities, well, unless the authority is corrupt. Even so if they are corrupted, often times we know that judgement will fall upon them sooner or later, why else then there’s so much news about it. If not yet, they are already facing the terror of being found out and the truth will be revealed. Of course there are some cases of people who were abused by authorities and that causes trauma but that’s another story. 

The context here is Paul wanted to tell the follower of Christ to keep doing what is good and righteous because now that Paul remove that clear boundary line of Jews and Gentiles, it created a risk for political status in the gentile community. It may lead to misunderstanding that their message may be twisted that they are no longer under the governing law of the Roman empire. If one does read the old testament especially book of Ezekiel and the land allotment, then it should be known that the responsibility of community, enforcement of laws and maintenance of order fall primarily to the price, the ideal ruler who govern alongside the priests and Levites in love of righteousness and justice. The system exist to uphold the community, to serve God and the people who live in the land. This is not to say that one should follow the authority when they ask you to do something go against God’s commandments. 

Paul urged the readers to love one another for that will fulfilled the law. When one loves another, they do not do something that harm the other and naturally they will not be breaking the commands of God such as adultery, murder, stealing, covet and any other commandments, hence summing up in this word loving neighbours as yourself, therefore love is the fulfilling of the law. May our Lord Jesus Christ help us to walk decently as in the day and to shun away from all evil and wickedness, to put on our Lord Jesus Christ by practising righteousness and love.
