Paul ended the first letter with directions of collection for the saints and it was very specific only to gather extras that they earned, that was Paul’s hearts to not be a burden to the church of Corinth and Galatia in order that when he and other missionaries arrived, they do not have to take collection. Then he told them about his travel plans and why he did not just want to pay a short visit during a passing through for he wanted more time with them. 

Paul also prepared beforehand the hearts of Corinth as so for Timothy and Apollos to be accepted and welcome into their church. It always amazed me and I feel even more grateful for God to allow the technology these days to be in a state where we can just book a hotel or meals or finding out what to expect when we travel. There was no instant messaging in the past and people had to wait for letters or informing each other using the postman. Nowadays, we have to make detailed planning months or years before to cope with more and instant spontaneous changes and be even more flexible than before. There’s a lot of decision making going on almost every minute of the day for a busy leader in church or in any field. There are two traits that Paul demonstrate in the times of change, that is first “if the Lord permits”, secondly he valued people more than anything else. Note that he follow the Lord’s will in his travel plans and he want to spend time with people and prepare people to accept other people. He was actually living a life of love God, love others in his mission trips and service to the Lord. 

The final greetings and exhortation of Paul is to keep alert, stand firm in the faith; be courageous; be strong and let all that you do be done in love. Then he commended the people who have contributed much in the kingdom of God. This is one of the most important part of the message, to do everything in love. May our Lord Jesus Christ help us all by His grace to achieve that.
