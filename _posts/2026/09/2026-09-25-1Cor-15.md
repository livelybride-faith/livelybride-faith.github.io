Our Lord Jesus Christ died on the cross and was raise on the third day, He appeared to Cephas, that’s Peter, and other disciples, the apostles and then lastly He appeared to Paul. Paul testified this towards all his teachings in church and it seems that some false teachings had arise and caused doubt to the people in the church of Corinth. He took the responsibility to correct that and to remind them that he was indeed a persecutor of the church of Christ and then believed because of his divine encounter with Christ in God’s grace. 

He then reminded them again how much harder he worked because he did in fact persecuted the church and this is a sign that when one feels convicted, they will try to rectify the wrongs and somehow wish that the situation would be redeemed. This does not give excuse to what happened did not matter, but in a way that redemption can be reached by the grace of God. Paul did that by putting his hope in Christ and he corrected the church of Corinth that indeed there is resurrection. The real danger of believing that there is no resurrection or hell or spiritual body after the death of physical body is that people will do whatever they want in this physical life and expect no consequences. However if one truly knows the truth that there’s a hell and resurrection in spiritual body, then it helps one to behave morally as well, to have self-control and not to indulge in immoral behaviors because we all shall stand before God in the judgement day. Whatever we sow now into the kingdom of God that is to produce the fruits of the spirit to which we become Christlike, all those that cannot be measured by physical materials but of mercy, kindness, generosity, love that was done out of a pure heart with compassion just like Jesus Christ, those are the treasures that are sown into the kingdom of heaven as it cannot be stolen nor wiped away. The kingdom of heaven is about the goodness and kindness that can be manifested thru the power of the Holy Spirit that has come upon a believer that empower them to feel convicted and be transformed where people would see and be astonished that this person has become another person, changed in their traits. Some will shun away because they could not accept it. Some will rejoice because they now see that God is real. It is important to stay in a fellowship that helps you to stay close to God and not merely performative in outward appearance. 

So Paul encouraged the church of Corinth to be steadfast, immovable, always excelling in the work of the Lord because you know that in the Lord your labor is not in vain. His meaning is for the people to continue in the hard work of producing fruits of the Spirit. 

>New Revised Standard Version: Updated Edition Galatians 5
> the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, generosity, faithfulness,* 23 gentleness, and self-control

May our Lord Jesus Christ help us all to grow in the spiritual journey of following Him and empower us to be able to produce the fruit of the Spirit.
