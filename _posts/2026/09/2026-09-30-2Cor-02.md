Paul wanted to make sure that the church in Corinth that read his second letter understood that it was for their sake but in turn also for his own sake because he cannot bear it if he cause them grief. He was a transformed person in Christ, previously before the encounter with Christ, he was someone that persecuted the church and caused them grief, after the encounter, he was filled by the Holy Spirit, so he may have been convicted by the prompting of the Holy Spirit when he was writing this letter. One can write any letter sarcastically or to shift blame such as you did not welcome me so I don’t have to come visit again. Paul did not ended things there, he made sure that he still cared about them and just didn’t want to cause any more trouble. 

He explained to them that any person who rejected him that caused him grief, to some extent has already caused it to the church of Corinth, because of that Paul could not visit them. He understood how a person felt when being outcasted like that and he did not want that person whoever that was to be outcasted forever. For Paul himself had receive forgiveness from our Lord Jesus Christ, accepted by Barnabas and then slowly by the other apostles and siblings in Christ. This time then, because he experienced that forgiveness and love, he was able to be life giving in such situation to those who opposed him as well. Truly…what goes around does comes around, but the point is that he was able to forgive because God forgave him and he chose to forgive when the time comes. 

This is in the context of some people in the church of Corinth rejected Paul’s teaching in the first letter. There are some matters that are not as important as the salvation of Christ and there are some matters involved rules and traditions that does not touch the fundamental things in the context of salvation. Hence he did not insist in the theological debates and arguments. He knew that this was the work of Satan, to cause confusion and division so that they could not be united and sin against God. He tried his best to not cause people to hate at all. He forgave but he also set a boundary in order that the conflicts can be minimized, he understood that some things in life especially when it revolves around people are not as simple and easy to be resolved or ignore. So he chose to stay within his boundary that he setup. 

May our Lord Jesus Christ helps us all to be sensitive to His heart and follow the prompting of the Holy Spirit in truth and to be able to forgive those who have rejected us due to the matters of theological debates, help us to understand His will and walk in His will.
