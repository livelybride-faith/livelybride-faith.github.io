Paul continue to address the subject of striving and this time surround the topic of spiritual gifts which all were given by the only God who created all beings. There are varieties of gifts, services and activities but it all derives from the same one Holy Spirit, our Lord Jesus Christ and it is our God who activates all of them in everyone. All these are for the benefits and building up of others in a community of Christ. 

1. Utterance of wisdom
2. Utterance of knowledge
3. faith
4. gifts of healing
5. working of powerful deeds
6. prophecy
7. discernment of spirits
8. various kinds of tongues
9. interpretation of tongues
Paul mentioned a total of nine gifts, but all are activated by one and the same Holy Spirit. 

Paul then emphasized a lot on the “One body, One Spirit, one God” breaking down into the body of Christ which is one but consist of many members, which means believers of Christ. Although there are many believers but we are all baptized into one body in Spirit. Paul used the body and limbs as analogy as it was quite helpful to understand that it’s relatable to people. For example, when our knee is injured and the pain caused weakness on the leg which then affect the other parts of the body like the hips or waist, we do not simply cut off the knee but find ways to cure it or rest and let it heal. This means that when a believer seem to be weaker than others, we do not outcast them. When they suffer, we suffer together, when they healed, we rejoice, when they succeed, we feel proud and honored together, rejoicing and celebrating together. This is how a church of God should look like instead of promoting striving and jealousy. 
God has appointed in the church according to the below order mentioned by Paul, it is not about who is better, stronger, more powerful or more authoritative, but it’s about the order of God in a church and all are needed for the building up of the body of Christ. 

1. Apostles
2. Prophets
3. Teachers
4. Deeds of power
5. Gifts of healing
6. Forms of assistance
7. Forms leadership
8. Various kinds of tongues

We are all gifted by the Holy Spirit, from one Lord, Jesus Christ, and all of it comes from our God. We all are equal and there’s no comparison at all because all are different and it’s illogical to compare apple with orange. This is why we need each other in a body of Christ. Paul said all these in the context of the matter of striving in a church or community of Christ. 

May our God activate the gifts that He has given to us and help us to use it according to His will and let us build each other up in the body of Christ in order to glorify God.
