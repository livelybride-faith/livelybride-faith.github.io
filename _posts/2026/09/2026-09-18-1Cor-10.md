Paul understood how his brothers and sisters of the Jewish community think and brought up in the history of Moses so he used the examples and tried to call to mind to them so that they do not fall into the trap of finding a loophole as he addressed the issue of idolatry and food. He had to be clear but also from the point of view of a principle not as rules and regulations to help them to truly understand that it is about love in the end. 

The thing about idolatry was never about the idols but he was implying that it was what they sacrifice was to demons and not to God. They gave their worship to demons not to God so when they eat from the altar they became partners and Paul did not want them to become partners of demons. Paul never restraint them forcefully and did not simply say if you eat this you will suffer calamity or stomachache, instead he said all things are permitted, but not all things are beneficial. All things are permitted but not all things build up. Do not seek your own advantage but that of the other. What he suggested was to live in peace and harmony without causing any commotions in the society or market to bring attention to this matter as maybe some of them kept asking around is this offered to idol, was that offered to idol? We must understand from the culture back then there wasn’t much restaurant or public cafes as of the modern era right now, their evangelism activities happens mostly at houses so if they were invited by unbelievers then they can eat whatever that was offered without questioning but of course if they mentioned that it was offered to the idols then do not take for the conscience’s sake, the host’s conscience not their own. For one’s freedom is not subject to the judgement of someone else’s conscience. This is why it was about love, it is for other’s sake that they can be build up or grow mature in spirit, it was for the glory of God. 

Paul encouraged the readers in Corinth to be imitators of him, as he is of Christ, which mean is to be imitators of Christ. He was trying to give them a physical, touchable figure so he made himself as an example because our Lord Jesus Christ has ascended to heaven and seated at the right hand of God, he spoke that for the sake of limitation in human mind so that they their brain can understand and fill in those blanks of how to behave. Human always learn by example and it speaks much louder than words. 
May our Lord Jesus Christ help us all that in whatever we eat, drink or whatever we do, do everything for the glory of God.
