Paul proceed to make it clearer because there was a discord among the faith community where they are arguing about things that did not relate to righteousness, peace and joy. The Jewish community wanted to follow Kosher’s rules and for Gentiles, they wanted to abolish the Kosher’s rules all together. So Paul stated clearly, do not judge each other, do note that this is in the context of rules & regulations on food but can be extended further that does not involve justice or righteousness. 

The principle is when we judge, we judge not by appearance but by righteous judgement. This is important for everyone to practice that we must discern when it is appropriate for us to pass judgement. Such as an appoint judge in court, they must discern even after being buried with evidences and arguments of defense and accusations, it is not easy at all. Just imagine when in a family, the parents decided to punish the elder sibling just because it’s more convenient and that it will stop the crying of the younger sibling. So the rivalry between the sibling start, but it doesn’t happen overnight, it will accumulate over time, and by the time that they become adult, the younger sibling would have been develop into a deluded personhood of doing everything without realizing there are consequences. The elder sibling would think that everything is their fault and unable to develop healthy boundaries in relationship, might be walked all over by others or have trust issues. Then what about the starting point of provocation? Hence this is require wise and righteous judgement. 

Back to the context, when we attend a Jewish wedding or gathering together, we were invited, right? That means we are friends and we don’t do bad things to our friends, we respect them and try to socialize with them to maintain peace and joy in the relationship. We don’t bring pork into their house nor mixing non kosher food into their gatherings because we love them. If we do that knowing that they believe in Kosher because they follow the commandment of God and provoke them in the sense of trying to make ourselves feel higher or better and thinking that they are still blinded, this is still rooted out of striving against each other. These matters are out of the scope in terms of righteousness and justice, but it does concern the matters of each belief and faith in God. So when it comes to doctrines of men, laws & rules of culture, sacrifices, feastings, seasons of holidays, cleansing, food, drink that does not break the commandment of God in terms of idolatry, all these are unworthy of debates. We respect each other by not provoking them so that they do not sin against God due to the pressure of doing things out of faith. We learn to live in harmony and peace. We do not overstep their boundary. That’s how we live in peace and joy in a society. If not then we become anti-social. This has to be taught in churches and by teachers patiently because there’s a gap of generation due to the covid social distancing period, they do not know how to be sociable or react or respond. Well, there are some who refuse to learn and just want to be anti-social intentionally for attention or just trying to find their footing, it’s important to discern and teach accordingly. 

So, let us therefore no longer pass judgement on one another but resolve instead never to put a stumbling block or hindrance in the way of a brother or sister. That’s what Paul urged us to do, the kingdom of God is not food and drink but righteousness and peace and joy in the Holy Spirit. May our Lord Jesus Christ help us all to pursue what makes for peace and for mutual upbuilding.
