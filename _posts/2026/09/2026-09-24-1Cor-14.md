Paul explained extensively regarding spiritual gifts of prophecy and tongues, in simple words, speaking in tongues is how one speaks to God and edifies self, prophecies build up the church which is to build up believers of Christ. He urged the church in Corinth to pursue the gift of prophecy more but he also would like people to speak in tongues and to pray for the power to interpret so that the church may be built up. 

For those who pray in tongues, pray in the mind, for those who sing praise with the spirit, sing praise with the mind also. This sentence is clearly stating that Christians should not pray like those who were just chanting meaninglessly away but when we engage in the activities of worship and prayer, we do it also with a clear intention from the heart that we are participating in a meaningful worship towards God. The whole context of this matter is about Paul leading the mind of the church in Corinth towards building up of each other, not tearing down. It seems that he was addressing the issues of disorders and chaos in the worship session in church, no matter it’s speaking in tongues, prophesy, preaching, singing hymns, one can only use their imagination that maybe at that time all of the activities were happening together at once and nothing make sense. Then there’s the issue of women should be silent in the churches, but take note that this is not the first occurence of Paul mentioning the word silent. Look at the verses below, it’s about the order and not superiority. There’s a pattern in it, it’s about the order. 
> New Revised Standard Version: Updated Edition 1 Corinthians 14
> 28 But if there is no one to interpret, let them be silent in church and speak to themselves and to God. 
> 29 Let two or three prophets speak, and let the others weigh what is said. 
> 30 If someone sitting receives a revelation, let the first person be silent.

It may be that there were striving amongst wife and husband as the sentence in verse 35 saying if there is something they want to learn, let them ask their husbands at home. Now go back to the era of the society in Corinth back then which originate from heavy idolatry activities and practices which created a lot of divination activities as well and there were a lot of false prophetess. Another argument is due to the mentioning of husband, it seems that there were some striving or challenging the husband by their wife as if they were in a home arguing. It happens sometimes when two become so familiar in a marriage to the point there’s no decency or basic respect anymore due to years of contempt and habitual interruption. It may root out of various underlying issues in a marriage but in a church worship setting, those who are leading the service must maintain order with one goal in mind that is to build up the church and to lead everyone to God, not to lead people into chaos or having to deal with awkwardness of the environment. 
Hence if there’s really some striving among serving couples in services, a leader should build relationship with them then try to understand what’s going on, was it because of stress, time, any challenges at home or work, if they need rest, let them rest; if therapy then suggest a trustworthy one. The end goal is to build up the church, people who are married are also part of the church. 

May our Lord Jesus Christ gives us wisdom and understanding, help us to discover our gifts and pursue them in a way that can edify the church and others in our lives.
