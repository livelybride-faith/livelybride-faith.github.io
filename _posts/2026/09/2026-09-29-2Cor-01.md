This letter was written after Paul’s visit, after the first letter of Corinthians. It seems that the church of Corinth did not receive it well and there were huge commotion that arise during his visit. It seems like Paul had experienced his first betrayal such as Christ’s, only betrayal from one’s own family or closed circles of community will feel this painful. Paul was the one that founded the church in Corinth, it can be said that he birth them, raised them, fed them like a father to a child. In the end, he was unwelcome in his own house, as if he has lost everything. This is to paint a picture of how deep the pain is for Paul not just on a physical level but also emotional and feelings. 

He was not the first one to experience and feel that pain of anguish but our Father in heaven, our God, the God of Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, the God of Israel had experienced it first hand during the time of Moses until now, where the old testament recorded of how the Israelite betrayed God by committing idolatry. It was even reenacted by Hosea with his experience of marrying a prostitute and be betrayed again but forgive and get her back. That’s who God is, His love is so deep for the people He created, the Israelites and the gentiles all the same, towards those who are willing to believe in Him, trust in Him and to provide second chances to them so that they could return back to God. This is in the context of divine forgiveness from God Himself, do not confuse it with abusing the grace of God by intentionally sin over and over again just to prove it or to think that there’s no consequences to one’s actions. So after this pain that Paul felt, he truly understood then there’s no reliance on anything or anyone but God. For those who have experience such betrayal and pain, something will change in the heart and mind, it is a like a giant asteroid shattering the foundation of trust towards human being and everything seems so fragile and worthless to the point that one will feel there’s no point living on earth anymore. It is like an explosion that wiped out all things that has been known to self and one will lose a lot of confidence in many things. It’s traumatic and it will take years if one even ever recover trust again. 

As dark as it may sound, there’s one light in this, during this affliction, if one can grasp hold of God, and put all trust in Him, that shall be a beautiful beginning of rebuilding trust with God, in God, truly, solely, relying on God. That is the beginning of a true foundation in truth. The process will take time, at first it will be filled with fear and terror but that’s the thing, it’s the only thing that push one to God for consolation and protection, because one truly knows only God can truly help. That is a great opportunity to build a deep unshakable relationship and communion with our God. Of course, there are practical steps such as worshipping God, praying, reading the bible, but usually just crying and weeping with deep anguish and pain from the heart begging God, praying to God, talking to God. Those are the times that God will always show up with comfort and consolation. One can say that it will be worst but also the best time in one’s lifetime. There will be time that one can only sigh deeply with an awe that said “God, you are truly God” when His console and ways of vindication being display before one’s very own eyes. There’s nothing but awe and surrender, this will lead to a deep worship towards God in humility. 

Paul tried to tell them in limited words or limited language but words can only tell so much, one must experience it to truly understand what he actually meant. Even so, he tried to explain to them and spare them that anguish of feeling rejected, he did not want them to feel that he rejected them, in fact he wanted them to know that he forgave, just like God did, just like our Lord Jesus Christ did. However, he did not want to cause any more trouble to the church, this was his heart. He just want them to be well, so he yielded and retreat for the sake of peace and harmony of the church. It was for God’s sake that he think of others before himself. He did not try to force himself to the church like a dictator nor did he try to manipulate them into accepting him just because he was the founder. God is also like that, He is the Maker, and Creator of every living being, yet He did not force or control people like dictator. He gave us freedom of choice. Without freedom of choice, there’s no true love. 

May our Lord Jesus Christ console all those who have experience betrayal and help us to rebuild the foundation of trust again in truth and in God this time. God is good and is our best comforter, let us rely on Him for He is faithful and merciful.
