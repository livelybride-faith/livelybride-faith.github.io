title: 1 Cor 2 - The true wisdom of God

---
Corinth was famously known for it’s scholarship and knowledge center for anyone who desire to learn, like a harvard of it’s own era. It’s a place full of excellent teachers and students whether worldly or godly, only time will tell. Time did tell, for Paul had to write two letters addressing the issues of sins and reminding them the gospel of Christ. 

They spoke eloquently with knowledge and wordings, such as we’ve seen in this era as well of how scholars quoted scriptures over and over again and talking down to others so that they feel superior. This rooted out of idolatry, greed and it grows into self-righteousness. A sinner know that they are a sinner and when one is already down, those who truly love God do not step on them, just like a bruised reed He will not break, a dimly burning wick He will not quench, He will faithfully bring forth justice. They have fallen into a delusion that by having knowledge or more certificates makes them more effective in their ministry or walk in faith. However the true wisdom of God does not comes from human interpretation, this does not mean that schools are useless. It is to emphasize that with much learning, they have lost sight of the true goal, that is to bring people to Christ and give them hope that they too are worthy of love from God. 

Sometimes this is not their fault, for they were brainwashed and influenced by other worldly scholars and systems, it’s not easy for them to get that out of their thought process. The fear of not belonging or outcasted, isolation and unable to rely on a certain community or losing the support of people will lead them to think that they have to choose a side. That’s how people fall into division. In fact, there’s only one side to choose, that is God. That’s why Paul wanted to help the church in Corinth to understand that, to have the mind of Christ. For if everyone has the mind of Christ, only then they can no longer be affected by the facade of having to choose a division. We may be tempted to judge them, but even at this era of 2026, we are not free from that, one just need to count the numbers of denominations in Christendom. It doesn’t mean that certain denominations is perfect or sinful, for there is not one human that is perfect or good except our Lord Jesus Christ. 

This does not mean that we do not address sins, but it’s important to know the condition of the flock before using a whip. One that has gone thru strict religious whipping in the past may be tempted to use that method more but Paul is an example of how he patiently lead the thoughts of the people that read his letters from the matters of head to heart. He did not quote scriptures just to put on more weight on his persuasions but he tried to relate with them, speaking their language and understanding what they need at that moment of weakness. He transformed their mind by rhetorical questions, and to bring humility back to their heart. His one goal is to help the church of Christ to align their hearts and foundation back to God. 
The settings were different from a classroom full of religious students eager to learn from a teacher where they were had a support system that they can rely on. Often times when we speak to a church of people, it’s full of mixed audiences and a shepherd need to speak according to the Holy Spirit’s guidance, not from a rigid knowledge set of answers. What does this person needs now that can help them to connect with God? This may be one of those things that Paul meant when he mentioned true wisdom of God. 

May our Lord Jesus Christ help us all to be more perceptive of His guidance and leading, may He help us so that we do not be conformed to this world but be transformed by the renewing of our mind in Christ and give us spiritual discernment in our walk in faith with God.
