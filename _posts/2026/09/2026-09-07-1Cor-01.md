This letter was written to the church of God in Corinth addressed by Paul as an apostle of Christ Jesus and he called the people who believed in God, saints. The title holds great weight and Paul did so intentionally so that they are reminded of who they are in God. Saints. 

The main purpose of this chapter is for Paul to address the issue of division in the church of Corinth specifically on the matters of baptism. He heard from Chloe’s people that they are quarrelling among themselves about who belongs to who. Then he asked them rhetorical questions, has Christ been divided? The believers were striving against each other in the house of God, which is not much different from church nowadays. They must have had performed great miracles or thriving as well, else they would not be trying to boast against each other that who is greater or who is lesser. Just as how Paul started the letter with “Just as the testimony of Christ has been strengthened among you, so that you are not lacking in any gift”, it was actually a reminder that each of their strength are gifts from God to partner with our Lord Jesus Christ for the sake of serving others. Each gifts are given, not earned, how can one then compare the gifts of who is greater, God is the giver, is He not? So everyone has their own strength and that’s why we need each other. Sometimes for this, sometimes for that, but it is not about who is stronger or who is weaker, it’s about serving God by helping each other in times of need. No one is perfect in this world, no one had all the power, gifts or talents in this world, only God. He is generous enough to share it with us and it’s foolish to compare with each other and feel superior or inferior towards each other. It’s not easy to avoid this in church, but as followers of Christ, we must always be reminded that God is God, we are the creation and to always stay humble that is to not be detached from reality : “Apart from God, we are nothing”. Instead of spending time quarreling, debating about who is better or worse, stronger or weaker which only leads to futility, we should fulfill our mission to share the gospel of Christ to non-believers and use our gifts to strengthen our brothers and sisters that leads to life. 

So if everyone is at the mercy of God, generously provided by God, sanctified by the blood of Christ, redeemed by the sacrificed of our Lord Jesus Christ on the cross, then no one can boast about all these except to give all glory to God. This is the gospel. Let us all give thanks to God and praise Him for all that He has done for us for He is worthy, let us be grateful for all that He has given us.
